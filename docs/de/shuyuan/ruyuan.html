Put your name on one day of the 2027 Analects calendarName a Day

The free side helps you understand; naming a day keeps your name.

Everything on Yikonglu stays free for everyone: the videos, the text, the plain renderings, the readers and the calendar. Naming a day sells no content. It does one thing: readers who want to support the work can put their name on one day of the Analects calendar.

What it is

From 1 January 2027, Yikonglu reads one passage of the Analects of Confucius each day: 365 lessons a year, the same passage on the same date every year. Name a day, and your name with a dedication of up to 20 Chinese characters (or 60 Latin letters) appears on that day's calendar entry and on the passage's page at yikonglu.com, for one year.

What you receive

  • The day is yours for a year: your name and dedication on the calendar and the passage page.
  • A keepsake card you can keep or give.
  • A place in the 2027 First Round list, published once it reaches 20 names.
  • One question a month answered by Yikonglu, publicly, under your name.

Price

  • Name a Day: US$29 for one year. The first hundred readers are founding members and can renew the same day at this price each year.
  • Name a Book: US$299 for one year. The Analects has twenty books; each can be named by one person only. Your name goes on that book's page and in the colophon of the 2027 complete reader.

Rules

  • One person per day, first come first served; renewals come first.
  • Write your dedication to someone, or to yourself. No business names, links, contact details or politics; we may ask you to change it.
  • Naming never touches the readings: every line stays checkable, and nothing is changed for anyone.
  • A screen name is enough. We do not need your real name or phone number.

Refunds

  • Full refund within 7 days of payment, no questions asked.
  • Full refund if the calendar stops before your day.
  • Full refund if we ask you to change your dedication and you prefer not to.

How

  1. Check the calendar (Chinese) to see which passage your day reads and whether it is taken.
  2. Order on Ko-fi and give the date (or book), the name to show and your dedication.
  3. Your keepsake card arrives within three days, and your name appears on the calendar.
