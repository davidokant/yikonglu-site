Hexagram 9 · Upper CanonXiǎo Xù ䷈

English translation

Judgment

Xiao Chu (Small Taming): success. Dense clouds but no rain, coming from our western outskirts.

Tuan Commentary

The Tuan says: In Xiao Chu the yielding line obtains its position, and those above and below respond to it. This is called Small Taming.

Strong and gentle, firm and central, so his will is carried out: hence success.

"Dense clouds but no rain": the yang is still moving on. "From our western outskirts": the bounty has not yet been dispensed.

Great Image

The Xiang says: Wind moves above heaven: Xiao Chu. The noble person accordingly refines his cultured virtue.

Nine in the first place

Nine in the first place: returning by his own way; what blame could there be? Good fortune.

Small Image "Returning by his own way": by rights this is good fortune.

Nine in the second place

Nine in the second place: drawn along in returning. Good fortune.

Small Image Drawn along in returning, he stays in the center; he too does not lose himself.

Nine in the third place

Nine in the third place: the spokes come off the carriage wheels. Husband and wife glare at each other.

Small Image "Husband and wife glare at each other": he cannot set his household right.

Six in the fourth place

Six in the fourth place: having sincerity, harm departs and fear goes away. No blame.

Small Image "Having sincerity, fear goes away": his will is at one with those above.

Nine in the fifth place

Nine in the fifth place: having sincerity, bound together; he shares his wealth with his neighbors.

Small Image "Having sincerity, bound together": he does not keep wealth to himself alone.

Nine at the top

Nine at the top: the rain has come, the taming is settled; virtue has accumulated to the full. For the wife, correctness is perilous. The moon is nearly full; for the noble person, setting out brings misfortune.

Small Image "The rain has come, it is settled": virtue has accumulated to the full. "For the noble person, setting out brings misfortune": there is something to be wary of.

Translated by Yikonglu, following Zhu Xi's reading.
