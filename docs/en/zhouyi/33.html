Hexagram 33 · Lower CanonDùn ䷠

English translation

Judgment

Dun (Retreat): success. A little benefit in being correct.

Tuan Commentary

The Tuan says: "Retreat, success": by retreating there is success. The firm is in its proper place and responded to; it moves with the times.

"A little benefit in being correct": the yin is gradually growing.

Great indeed is the meaning of the time of Retreat!

Great Image

The Xiang says: A mountain beneath heaven: Dun. The noble person accordingly keeps petty people at a distance, not with hatred but with dignity.

Six in the first place

Six in the first place: retreating at the tail: perilous. Do not try to go anywhere.

Small Image The peril of retreating at the tail: if he does not go, what disaster can there be?

Six in the second place

Six in the second place: held fast with the hide of a yellow ox; no one can loosen it.

Small Image "Held with a yellow ox": his resolve is firm.

Nine in the third place

Nine in the third place: tied in retreat: there is affliction and peril. Keeping servants and maids brings good fortune.

Small Image The peril of being tied in retreat: affliction and exhaustion. "Keeping servants and maids brings good fortune": great affairs are not possible.

Nine in the fourth place

Nine in the fourth place: retreating despite fondness: for the noble person, good fortune; not so for the petty person.

Small Image The noble person retreats despite fondness; the petty person cannot.

Nine in the fifth place

Nine in the fifth place: admirable retreat. Correctness brings good fortune.

Small Image "Admirable retreat, correctness brings good fortune": he sets his will right.

Nine at the top

Nine at the top: carefree retreat. Nothing is not beneficial.

Small Image "Carefree retreat, nothing not beneficial": he has nothing to doubt.

Translated by Yikonglu, following Zhu Xi's reading.
