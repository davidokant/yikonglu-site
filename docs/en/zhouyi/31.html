Hexagram 31 · Lower CanonXián ䷞

English translation

Judgment

Xian (Influence): success; beneficial to be correct. Taking a wife brings good fortune.

Tuan Commentary

The Tuan says: Xian means to influence.

The yielding is above and the firm below; the two energies stir and respond to each other; halting and pleased, the man places himself below the woman. Hence "success, beneficial to be correct; taking a wife brings good fortune."

Heaven and earth influence each other and the myriad things are transformed and born; the sage influences the hearts of people and all under heaven is at peace. Contemplate what they influence, and the nature of heaven, earth and all things can be seen.

Great Image

The Xiang says: A lake upon the mountain: Xian. The noble person accordingly receives others with an open mind.

Six in the first place

Six in the first place: influence in the big toe.

Small Image "Influence in the big toe": his will is directed outward.

Six in the second place

Six in the second place: influence in the calves: misfortune. Staying put brings good fortune.

Small Image "Though misfortune, staying put brings good fortune": compliance does no harm.

Nine in the third place

Nine in the third place: influence in the thighs; he holds to what he follows. Going on brings regret.

Small Image "Influence in the thighs": he too cannot stay still. His will is set on following others; what he holds to is low.

Nine in the fourth place

Nine in the fourth place: correctness brings good fortune; remorse vanishes. If restlessly coming and going, only your friends follow your thoughts.

Small Image "Correctness brings good fortune, remorse vanishes": he has not yet been harmed by influence. "Restlessly coming and going": not yet radiant and great.

Nine in the fifth place

Nine in the fifth place: influence in the flesh of the back. No remorse.

Small Image "Influence in the back": his aim is trivial.

Six at the top

Six at the top: influence in the jaws, cheeks and tongue.

Small Image "Influence in the jaws, cheeks and tongue": he lets his mouth run on.

Translated by Yikonglu, following Zhu Xi's reading.
