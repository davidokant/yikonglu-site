Hexagram 3 · Upper CanonZhūn ䷂

English translation

Judgment

Zhun: great success; it is beneficial to be correct. Do not set out to go anywhere. It is beneficial to establish feudal lords.

Tuan Commentary

The Tuan says: Zhun: the firm and the soft first meet, and difficulty is born.

Moving in the midst of danger: great success, with correctness.

The movement of thunder and rain fills everything; heaven makes things amid chaos and darkness. It is fitting to establish lords, but not yet to think all is at peace.

Great Image

The Xiang says: Clouds and thunder: Zhun. The noble person, following it, sets the world in order.

Nine in the first place

Nine in the first place: hesitating to advance. It is beneficial to abide in correctness. It is beneficial to be established as a lord.

Small Image Though hesitating, his will is set on acting correctly. Noble yet placing himself below the humble, he greatly wins the people.

Six in the second place

Six in the second place: in difficulty, turning back; riding a horse, yet holding back. Not robbers, but suitors. The maiden is correct and does not consent to marry; after ten years she consents.

Small Image The difficulty of six in the second place is that it rides upon the firm. "After ten years she consents": a return to the norm.

Six in the third place

Six in the third place: chasing deer without a forester, one only goes deep into the forest. The noble person, seeing the sign, had better give up; going on brings humiliation.

Small Image "Chasing deer without a forester": being bent on the game. The noble person gives it up, for going on brings humiliation and exhaustion.

Six in the fourth place

Six in the fourth place: riding a horse, holding back; seeking a marriage. Going brings good fortune; nothing is not beneficial.

Small Image Seeking and then going: this is clear-sighted.

Nine in the fifth place

Nine in the fifth place: his bounty is stored up and blocked. In small matters, correctness brings good fortune; in great matters, even correctness brings misfortune.

Small Image "His bounty is blocked": his giving does not yet shine.

Six at the top

Six at the top: riding a horse, holding back; weeping tears of blood in streams.

Small Image "Weeping tears of blood in streams": how could this last long!

Translated by Yikonglu, following Zhu Xi's reading.
