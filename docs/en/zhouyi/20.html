Hexagram 20 · Upper CanonGuàn ䷓

English translation

Judgment

Guan (Contemplation): having washed the hands, before presenting the offering; sincere and reverent in bearing.

Tuan Commentary

The Tuan says: The great one to be contemplated is above; compliant and gentle, central and correct, he is contemplated by all under heaven.

"Having washed but not yet presenting, sincere and reverent": those below look on and are transformed.

Contemplate the spirit-like way of heaven, and the four seasons do not deviate. The sage establishes teaching by this spirit-like way, and all under heaven submit.

Great Image

The Xiang says: Wind moves over the earth: Guan. The former kings accordingly inspected the regions, observed the people and set up teachings.

Six in the first place

Six in the first place: a child's view. For the petty person no blame; for the noble person, regret.

Small Image The first six, a child's view: this is the way of petty people.

Six in the second place

Six in the second place: peeping through the door. It is beneficial for a woman to be correct.

Small Image "Peeping, a woman's correctness": for a man, this too would be shameful.

Six in the third place

Six in the third place: contemplating my own life, to advance or retreat.

Small Image "Contemplating my own life, to advance or retreat": he has not lost the way.

Six in the fourth place

Six in the fourth place: contemplating the glory of the state. It is beneficial to be a guest of the king.

Small Image "Contemplating the glory of the state": he aspires to be a guest at court.

Nine in the fifth place

Nine in the fifth place: contemplating my own life. For the noble person, no blame.

Small Image "Contemplating my own life": that is, looking at the people.

Nine at the top

Nine at the top: contemplating his life. For the noble person, no blame.

Small Image "Contemplating his life": his mind is not yet at ease.

Translated by Yikonglu, following Zhu Xi's reading.
