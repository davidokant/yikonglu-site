Hexagram 56 · Lower CanonLǚ ䷷

English translation

Judgment

Lü (The Traveller): small success. For the traveller, correctness brings good fortune.

Tuan Commentary

The Tuan says: "The traveller: small success": the yielding attains the centre in the outer trigram and complies with the firm; still, it clings to brightness. Therefore "small success; for the traveller, correctness brings good fortune."

Great indeed is the timely meaning of Lü!

Great Image

The Xiang says: On the mountain there is fire: Lü. The noble person accordingly is clear-sighted and careful in applying punishments, and does not let lawsuits linger.

Six in the first place

Six in the first place: the traveller is petty and mean; thus he brings calamity on himself.

Small Image "The traveller is petty and mean": his will is exhausted, and calamity follows.

Six in the second place

Six in the second place: the traveller reaches an inn, carrying his means with him, and gains the loyalty of a servant boy.

Small Image "He gains the loyalty of a servant boy": in the end there is no fault.

Nine in the third place

Nine in the third place: the traveller's inn burns down, and he loses his servant boy. Even with correctness, danger.

Small Image "The traveller's inn burns down": that too is harm enough. To treat those below as a traveller does: by rights he loses them.

Nine in the fourth place

Nine in the fourth place: the traveller finds a place to stay and gains his means and an axe, but my heart is not glad.

Small Image "The traveller finds a place to stay": he has not yet attained his position. "He gains his means and an axe": his heart is not yet glad.

Six in the fifth place

Six in the fifth place: shooting a pheasant, he loses one arrow; in the end he gains praise and a commission.

Small Image "In the end he gains praise and a commission": it reaches up to those above.

Nine at the top

Nine at the top: the bird's nest is burned; the traveller first laughs, then wails and weeps. He loses his ox through carelessness: misfortune.

Small Image To be a traveller and at the top: by rights he is burned. "He loses his ox through carelessness": in the end no one tells him of it.

Translated by Yikonglu, following Zhu Xi's reading.
