Hexagram 62 · Lower CanonXiǎo Guò ䷽

English translation

Judgment

Xiaoguo (Small Excess): success. It is beneficial to be correct. Small affairs may be done; great affairs may not. The flying bird leaves behind its call: it is not fitting to go up, it is fitting to go down; great good fortune.

Tuan Commentary

The Tuan says: Xiaoguo: the small exceeds, and there is success.

Exceeding, yet beneficial in correctness: it moves with the times. The yielding attains the centre: therefore small affairs bring good fortune.

The firm has lost its position and is not central: therefore great affairs may not be done.

It has the image of a flying bird. "The flying bird leaves behind its call: not fitting to go up, fitting to go down; great good fortune": going up is contrary, going down is compliant.

Great Image

The Xiang says: On the mountain there is thunder: Xiaoguo. The noble person accordingly errs toward excess of respect in conduct, excess of grief in mourning, and excess of thrift in spending.

Six in the first place

Six in the first place: the bird flies, and thereby meets misfortune.

Small Image "The bird flies, and thereby meets misfortune": nothing can be done about it.

Six in the second place

Six in the second place: passing by the grandfather, he meets the grandmother; not reaching the ruler, he meets the minister. No blame.

Small Image "Not reaching the ruler": a minister must not overstep.

Nine in the third place

Nine in the third place: not taking extra care to guard against it, if he goes along he may be slain: misfortune.

Small Image "If he goes along he may be slain": what misfortune!

Nine in the fourth place

Nine in the fourth place: no blame. Not exceeding, he meets what fits. Going forth is dangerous; he must be on guard. Do not cling to lasting correctness.

Small Image "Not exceeding, he meets what fits": his position is not fitting. "Going forth is dangerous; he must be on guard": in the end it cannot last. (Zhu Xi says the meaning here is unclear and should be left in doubt.)

Six in the fifth place

Six in the fifth place: dense clouds but no rain, from our western outskirts. The duke shoots with a corded arrow and takes the one in the cave.

Small Image "Dense clouds but no rain": it is already too high.

Six at the top

Six at the top: not meeting what fits, it overshoots; the flying bird departs: misfortune. This is called calamity and disaster.

Small Image "Not meeting what fits, it overshoots": it is already overweening.

Translated by Yikonglu, following Zhu Xi's reading.
