Hexagram 12 · Upper CanonPǐ ䷋

English translation

Judgment

Pi (Obstruction): (Zhu Xi takes "of the wrong people" as an interpolation.) It does not benefit the noble person's correctness. The great departs, the small arrives.

Tuan Commentary

The Tuan says: "Obstruction... does not benefit the noble person's correctness; the great departs, the small arrives": this means heaven and earth do not interact and things do not flow; high and low do not interact and the realm has no true states. Yin within and yang without, yielding within and firm without, petty people within and noble people without: the way of the petty grows, the way of the noble wanes.

Great Image

The Xiang says: Heaven and earth do not interact: Pi. The noble person accordingly keeps his virtue modest to avoid trouble, and cannot be honored with salary.

Six in the first place

Six in the first place: pulling up a cogon reed, the roots come with it, together with its kind. Correctness brings good fortune and success.

Small Image "Pulling up the reed, correctness brings good fortune": his will is devoted to the ruler.

Six in the second place

Six in the second place: enfolding and complying: for the petty person, good fortune; the great person keeps to obstruction, and thus succeeds.

Small Image "The great person keeps to obstruction and succeeds": he does not mix with the crowd.

Six in the third place

Six in the third place: harboring shame.

Small Image "Harboring shame": his position is improper.

Nine in the fourth place

Nine in the fourth place: there is a mandate; no blame. His companions share in the blessing.

Small Image "There is a mandate, no blame": his will is carried out.

Nine in the fifth place

Nine in the fifth place: obstruction comes to rest; for the great person, good fortune. "It may perish, it may perish!": thus it is tied to a clump of mulberry.

Small Image The good fortune of the great person: his position is exactly right.

Nine at the top

Nine at the top: obstruction overturned; first obstruction, then joy.

Small Image When obstruction reaches its end it overturns: how could it last long!

Translated by Yikonglu, following Zhu Xi's reading.
