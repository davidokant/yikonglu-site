Sequence of the Hexagrams · Sequence of the HexagramsThere are heaven and earth, and then the myriad things are b

English translation

There are heaven and earth, and then the myriad things are born. What fills the space between heaven and earth is nothing but the myriad things; hence Zhun follows. Zhun means fullness; Zhun is the first birth of things. Things when born are sure to be immature; hence Meng follows. Meng is immaturity, the young state of things. Young things must be nourished; hence Xu follows. Xu is the way of food and drink. Food and drink are sure to bring disputes; hence Song follows. Disputes are sure to raise the multitude; hence Shi follows. Shi is the multitude. The multitude is sure to draw together; hence Bi follows. Bi is drawing together. Drawing together is sure to bring some accumulation; hence Xiao Xu follows. When things are accumulated there is ritual; hence Lü follows. Treading the path and then at peace

only then is there security; hence Tai follows. Tai is free flow. Things cannot flow freely forever; hence Pi follows. Things cannot stay blocked forever; hence Tong Ren follows. To one who is one with others, things are sure to come home; hence Da You follows. One who possesses much must not be overfull; hence Qian follows. One who possesses much and can be modest is sure to be at ease; hence Yu follows. Ease is sure to have followers; hence Sui follows. One who follows others with gladness is sure to have affairs; hence Gu follows. Gu is affairs. When there are affairs one can become great; hence Lin follows. Lin is greatness. When things are great they can be looked upon; hence Guan follows. What can be looked upon then finds union; hence Shi He follows. He is union. Things cannot simply unite carelessly; hence Bi follows. Bi is adornment. When adornment is carried to the end, success is exhausted; hence Bo follows. Bo is stripping away. Things cannot be stripped away forever; when stripping reaches the top it returns to the bottom; hence Fu follows. With return there is no falsehood; hence Wu Wang follows. With freedom from falsehood there can be accumulation; hence Da Xu follows. When things are accumulated they can be nourished; hence Yi follows. Yi is nourishment. Without nourishment there can be no movement; hence Da Guo follows. Things cannot be in excess forever; hence Kan follows. Kan is the pit. One who falls into a pit is sure to cling to something; hence Li follows. Li is clinging.

There are heaven and earth, and then there are the myriad things; there are the myriad things, and then there are male and female; there are male and female, and then there are husband and wife; there are husband and wife, and then there are father and son; there are father and son, and then there are ruler and minister; there are ruler and minister, and then there are high and low; there are high and low, and then ritual and rightness have their place. The way of husband and wife must not be short-lived; hence Heng follows. Heng is duration. Things cannot stay in one place forever; hence Dun follows. Dun is retreat. Things cannot retreat forever; hence Da Zhuang follows. Things cannot be vigorous forever; hence Jin follows. Jin is advance. Advance is sure to meet injury; hence Ming Yi follows. Yi is injury. One injured abroad is sure to return home; hence Jia Ren follows. When the way of the family is exhausted there is sure to be estrangement; hence Kui follows. Kui is estrangement. Estrangement is sure to bring hardship; hence Jian follows. Jian is hardship. Things cannot be in hardship forever; hence Xie follows. Xie is release. Release is sure to bring some loss; hence Sun follows. Decrease without end is sure to bring increase; hence Yi follows. Increase without end is sure to break through; hence Guai follows. Guai is breakthrough. Breakthrough is sure to bring an encounter; hence Gou follows. Gou is encounter.

When things meet they then gather; hence Cui follows. Cui is gathering. Gathering and rising upward is called ascent; hence Sheng follows. Ascent without end is sure to bring exhaustion; hence Kun follows. One exhausted above is sure to return below; hence Jing follows. The way of the well must be renewed; hence Ge follows. For transforming things nothing compares with the cauldron; hence Ding follows. For presiding over the sacred vessels nothing compares with the eldest son; hence Zhen follows. Zhen is movement. Things cannot move forever, so they are stopped; hence Gen follows. Gen is stopping. Things cannot stop forever; hence Jian follows. Jian is advance. Advance is sure to find a home; hence Gui Mei follows. One who finds a home is sure to become great; hence Feng follows. Feng is greatness. One who exhausts greatness is sure to lose his dwelling; hence Lü follows. A traveler has nowhere to be received; hence Xun follows. Xun is entering. Having entered, one is glad; hence Dui follows. Dui is gladness. After gladness comes dispersal; hence Huan follows. Huan is dispersal. Things cannot be dispersed forever; hence Jie follows. With limitation comes trust; hence Zhong Fu follows. One who has trust is sure to act on it; hence Xiao Guo follows. One who surpasses others is sure to succeed; hence Ji Ji follows. Things cannot be exhausted; hence Wei Ji follows, and there it ends.
