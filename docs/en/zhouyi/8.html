Hexagram 8 · Upper CanonBì ䷇

English translation

Judgment

Bi (Holding Together): good fortune. Divine again to examine yourself; if there is greatness, constancy and correctness, no blame. Those not at peace now come; the latecomer meets misfortune.

Tuan Commentary

The Tuan says: Bi means good fortune.

Bi means support: those below comply and follow.

"Divine again: greatness, constancy, correctness, no blame": because he is firm and central. "Those not at peace now come": above and below respond to him. "The latecomer meets misfortune": his way is exhausted.

Great Image

The Xiang says: Water upon the earth: Bi. The former kings accordingly established the myriad states and kept close to the feudal lords.

Six in the first place

Six in the first place: holding to him with sincerity, no blame. Sincerity filling a plain earthen jar: in the end others come, and there is unexpected good fortune.

Small Image The first six of Bi: there is unexpected good fortune.

Six in the second place

Six in the second place: holding to him from within; correctness brings good fortune.

Small Image "Holding to him from within": he does not lose himself.

Six in the third place

Six in the third place: holding to the wrong people.

Small Image "Holding to the wrong people": is that not hurtful?

Six in the fourth place

Six in the fourth place: holding to him from without; correctness brings good fortune.

Small Image Holding from without to the worthy one: thus he follows his superior.

Nine in the fifth place

Nine in the fifth place: open holding together. The king hunts on three sides only and lets the game ahead escape; the townspeople need no warning. Good fortune.

Small Image The good fortune of open holding: his position is correct and central. Letting go those who turn away and taking those who comply: that is letting the game ahead escape. The townspeople need no warning: the ruler employs them by the middle way.

Six at the top

Six at the top: holding together without a head. Misfortune.

Small Image "Holding together without a head": there is nothing to end in.

Translated by Yikonglu, following Zhu Xi's reading.
