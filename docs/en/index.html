Recently added

  1. 5.18Not even this counts as ren?Loyalty and purity are single virtues; ren asks whether one fully accords with principle and truly has no selfish mind. Trusting small things to vouch for great ones was Zizhang's fault
  2. 5.17Was Zang Wenzhong really clever?Not putting his mind to what the people needed, and courting spirits instead, does not count as wisdom
  3. 5.16Is it more polite not to stand on ceremony with old friends?Respect fading with time is the usual course; keeping it is what “good at associating with others” means
  4. 5.15Zichan had four ways of the gentlemanPraising point by point actually shows there is still somewhere he has not reached. Respectful to himself, reverent to superiors, kind to the people, just in employing them: each of the four has its own object. Judging a person by one phrase or one event is wrong
  5. 5.14Why was Kong Wenzi called “wen”?Zigong asked because Kong Wenzi's reputation was poor. Confucius answered only with this one rule for posthumous names, neither defending him wholesale nor erasing his merit; “wen” goes only this far, not as far as ordering heaven and earth
  6. 5.13When Zilu heard somethingWhat he feared was not learning more, but the last thing not yet done. His fellow students recorded it because they felt they fell short
