The Analects 5.25Why was Confucius' wish greater than Zilu's and Yan Yuan's?
颜渊、季路侍。子曰：盍各言尔志？子路曰：愿车马、衣轻裘，与朋友共。敝之而无憾。颜渊曰：愿无伐善，无施劳。子路曰：愿闻子之志。子曰：老者安之，朋友信之，少者怀之。

All three share with others, differing in scale: Zilu shares carriage and horses with friends, Yan Yuan does not boast or show off, Confucius lets each find their place, like heaven and earth nurturing without a trace. Today: the auntie in the housing block whom the elderly trust with their keys and to whose door the children run first after school.

Recently read

  1. 6.19Was Confucius looking down on people?This is not ranking people, but fitting words to the listener: speak too high and the listener cannot catch it, and harm is done. Teach according to capacity, and climb step by step.
  2. 6.18Knowing, liking, delighting: what's the difference?Knowing there is this bowl of rice, loving to eat it, and having eaten your fill and being at ease: three levels. The difference is not how much you know, but whether you have actually gained it.
  3. 6.17Don't crooked people live perfectly well too?People are meant to live upright; the crooked are alive too, not because they deserve it but because they have got away with it by luck.
  4. 6.16Too rough or too polished: which would Confucius prefer?Too much plainness is crude, too much ornament is hollow; only the two in balance make a gentleman. If one must lean, better lean plain: with the base intact, refinement can be added; with the base gone, refinement has nowhere to go.
  5. 6.15Everyone goes out by the door: why not by the right way?Everyone knows to leave by the door rather than climb the wall; the right road in life lies right in front of us, yet people will not take it. It is not that the Way is far from them; they have wandered away themselves.
