子张第十九 · chapter 20 · not yet coveredZhou's wickedness was not as extreme as they say

The video for this chapter is still in production. For now this page gives the original text, the facsimile page, a working translation and the commentary; the full reading follows when the video is out.

白Plain Chinese

子贡说，纣的恶并没有传说中那么重，因此君子厌恶把自己置于污下之地，否则天下的恶名都会汇聚到他身上。

Draft plain-Chinese rendering following Zhu Xi; it will be checked when the chapter is covered.
