雍也第六 · chapter 19Was Confucius looking down on people?

What this chapter says

This is not ranking people, but fitting words to the listener: speak too high and the listener cannot catch it, and harm is done. Teach according to capacity, and climb step by step.

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：中等以上资质的人，可以跟他讲高深的道理；中等以下的，就不能跟他讲高深的。

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

常The common reading

Is Confucius looking down on people here?

注Key words in the commentary

“语，告也” — “yu” means to tell; “言教人者，当随其高下而告语之，则其言易入而无躐等之弊也” — one who teaches should speak according to the learner's level; then the words go in easily and there is no fault of skipping steps; “上” is read shàng.

说What other commentators say

Zhang Jingfu: “圣人之道，精粗虽无二致，但其施教，则必因其材而笃焉……使之切问近思，而渐进于高远也” — the sage's Way is one in its fine and coarse parts, but in teaching he always builds on each person's capacity … making them ask closely and reflect on what is near, and so advance gradually to the high and far.
