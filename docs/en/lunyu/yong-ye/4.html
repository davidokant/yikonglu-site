雍也第六 · chapter 4Can someone of bad background be employed?

白Plain Chinese

孔子谈到仲弓，说：耕牛生的小牛，毛色纯红，两角端正，就算人们嫌它出身不想用，山川的神灵也不会不要它吧？

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

常The common reading

Can a person of poor background be employed?

注Key words in the commentary

“犁，利之反” — “li” is read lí, brindled; “骍，息营反” — “xing” is read xīng, red; “舍，上声” — “she” in the rising tone; “言人虽不用，神必不舍也” — though people may not use it, the spirits will surely not reject it.

家What other commentators say

Zhu Xi: “仲弓父贱而行恶，故夫子以此譬之……然此论仲弓云尔，非与仲弓言也” — Zhonggong's father was lowly and behaved badly, so the Master used this comparison … but this is speaking about Zhonggong, not speaking to him. Fan: “以瞽瞍为父而有舜，以鲧为父而有禹……子能改父之过，变恶以为美，则可谓孝矣” — with Gusou as father there was Shun, with Gun as father there was Yu … a son who can correct his father's faults and turn bad into good may be called filial.

读How Yikonglu reads it

A calf of a brindled plough-ox, pure red with well-formed horns: even if people will not sacrifice it because of its origin, the spirits of hills and rivers will not reject it. A father's badness cannot cancel a son's goodness; Zhu Xi notes specifically that this speaks about Zhonggong, not to his face.
