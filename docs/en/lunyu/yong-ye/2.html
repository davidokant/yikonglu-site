雍也第六 · chapter 2Why is Confucius' “love of learning” not about reading a lot?

白Plain Chinese

哀公问孔子：弟子当中哪个称得上好学？孔子回答：有个叫颜回的好学，生气不迁到别人身上，同样的过错不犯第二回。可惜命短，已经死了！如今再没有这样的人，没听说还有谁好学。

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

常The common reading

Why does Confucius' “love of learning” not mean reading a lot?

注Key words in the commentary

“好” in the departing tone; “亡，与无同” — “wang” is the same as “none”; “迁，移也。贰，复也” — “qian” is to move, “er” is to repeat; Yan Hui “三十二而卒” — died at thirty-two (as Zhu Xi records).

家What other commentators say

Master Cheng: “颜子之怒，在物不在己，故不迁。有不善未尝不知，知之未尝复行，不贰过也” — Yanzi's anger lay in the thing, not in himself, so it did not shift; whenever there was something not good he always knew it, and having known it never did it again: that is not repeating a fault; “喜怒在事，则理之当喜怒者也，不在血气则不迁” — when joy and anger lie in the matter, they are what principle calls for; not lying in the blood and breath, they do not shift; “如鉴之照物，妍媸在彼，随物应之而已” — like a mirror reflecting things, beauty and ugliness are in them; it simply responds to each. Master Zhang: “慊于己者，不使萌于再” — what dissatisfies oneself is not allowed to sprout a second time. Master Cheng on learning: “学以至乎圣人之道” — learning is to reach the Way of the sage; “约其情使合于中，正其心，养其性” — restrain the feelings to accord with the mean, rectify the heart, nourish the nature; and he ends mocking “今人乃谓圣本生知，非学可至，而所以为学者，不过记诵文辞之间，其亦异乎颜子之学矣” — people now say sagehood is innate and cannot be reached by learning, and their learning is nothing but memorising and composing — far from Yanzi's learning.

读How Yikonglu reads it

Duke Ai asks about learning; Confucius answers with two matters of self-cultivation. Anger arises with the matter and stops when it is over, not moved on to others; a fault made once is known and not made again. Saying that since Yan Hui's death he has heard of no one who loves learning shows that love of learning lies in governing one's feelings, not in reading much. Today: scolded at work, he still crouches down at the door to tie his child's shoelaces properly.
