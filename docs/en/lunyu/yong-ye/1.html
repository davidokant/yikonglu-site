雍也第六 · chapter 1Why can one's affairs be simple but not one's heart?

What this chapter says

Two kinds of “simple”: one with a master within, reverent everywhere, so affairs are naturally uncluttered; the other first intends to save effort and lets even oneself off, which becomes too simple. Confucius grants Zhonggong the south-facing seat; Zhonggong uses the question about Bozi to draw the distinction, and Confucius says “Yong's words are right.” Today: the team with the fewest rules is the one whose leader is the most punctual and orderly.

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：冉雍呀，是可以坐南面、主一方之政的人。仲弓问子桑伯子为人如何。孔子说：还可以，他行事简省。仲弓说：自己存心恭敬，办事简省，用这样的态度来面对百姓，不也行吗？要是存心随便，办事又简省，岂不是太简了？孔子说：冉雍的话对。

常The common reading

Why can affairs be handled simply, but the heart must not be simple?

注Key words in the commentary

“南面者，人君听治之位” — facing south is the ruler's seat for hearing government; “可者，仅可而有所未尽之辞。简者，不烦之谓” — “will do” means barely acceptable, with something lacking; “simple” means not fussy; the “大” of “大简” is read tài; the “衣” of “不衣冠而处” is in the departing tone, to wear. Zisang Bozi, Hu suspects, is the Zisang Hu mentioned in Zhuangzi.

说What other commentators say

Zhu Xi: “言自处以敬，则中有主而自治严……岂不失之大简，而无法度之可守乎” — if one holds oneself in reverence, there is a master within and self-discipline is strict … otherwise is it not too simple, with no rules to keep? He cites the Kongzi Jiayu: Bozi “不衣冠而处，夫子讥其欲同人道于牛马” — lived without proper clothes and cap; the Master mocked him for wanting to make human ways like those of oxen and horses; and Master Cheng: “居敬则心中无物，故所行自简；居简则先有心于简，而多一简字矣，故曰大简” — dwelling in reverence, the heart holds nothing extra, so action is naturally simple; dwelling in simplicity, one first sets one's mind on simplicity, adding one more “simple”, hence “too simple”.

Compared with Zhu Xi

CommentaryPointRelationConfidenceNote
杨伯峻《论语译注》南面word sensesettledYang 6.1, 南面: Yang takes it broadly as the seat of any chief, from the Son of Heaven and lords to ministers and grandees; Zhu specifically as the ruler's seat for hearing government, showing Zhonggong had a ruler's capacity; the scope differs. Yang 6.2, 简: Yang explicitly adopts Zhu's “affairs not troublesome, the people not disturbed”; Yang does not adopt identifications of Zisang Bozi such as Zisang Hu, and Zhu records Hu's guess that he was Zisang Hu without proof.
钱穆《论语新解》samefairly sureBoth: 南面 is the ruler's seat, 可 is barely acceptable, 简 is not troublesome; both separate dwelling in reverence and acting simply from dwelling in simplicity and acting simply, approving strict self-discipline that does not trouble the people.
