雍也第六 · chapter 10He said he lacked the strength: why didn't Confucius believe him?

白Plain Chinese

冉求说：不是不喜欢老师讲的道，是我力气不够。孔子说：力气真不够的人，走到半路才倒下。你现在是自己先画了一道线，停在线里不走。

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

常The common reading

He said his strength was not enough: why did Confucius not believe him?

注Key words in the commentary

“力不足者，欲进而不能” — lacking strength means wanting to advance but being unable; “画者，能进而不欲，谓之画者，如画地以自限也” — drawing a line means being able to advance but not wanting to, like drawing a line on the ground to limit oneself; “女” is read rǔ, “you”.

家What other commentators say

Hu: “夫子称颜回不改其乐，冉求闻之，故有是言。然使求说夫子之道，诚如口之说刍豢，则必将尽力以求之……此冉求之所以局于艺也” — the Master praised Yan Hui for not changing his joy; Ran Qiu heard it and so said this. But had Qiu truly delighted in the Master's Way as the mouth delights in fine meat, he would surely have exerted all his strength to pursue it … this is why Ran Qiu stayed confined to skills.

读How Yikonglu reads it

Someone truly lacking strength collapses halfway along the road; Ran Qiu drew a line on the ground before setting out. It is not that he cannot, but that he will not.
