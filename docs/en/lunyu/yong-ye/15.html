雍也第六 · chapter 15Everyone goes out by the door: why not by the right way?

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：谁出屋能不经过门呢？为什么就没有人走这条道呢？

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

常The common reading

Everyone leaves by the door: why will people not walk the right road in life?

注Key words in the commentary

“言人不能出不由户，何故乃不由此道邪？怪而叹之之辞” — no one can go out except by the door, so why do people not follow this Way? Words of wonder and lament.

家What other commentators say

Hong: “人知出必由户，而不知行必由道。非道远人，人自远尔” — people know that going out must be by the door, but not that conduct must follow the Way. It is not that the Way is far from people; people distance themselves from it.

读How Yikonglu reads it

Everyone knows to leave by the door rather than climb the wall; the right road in life lies right in front of us, yet people will not take it. It is not that the Way is far from them; they have wandered away themselves.
