雍也第六 · chapter 8Why did Confucius hold his dying student's hand through the window?

What this chapter says

Boniu's family moved the sickbed under the south window, as for a ruler's visit; Confucius did not dare accept that honour, so he did not enter and held his hand through the window in farewell. “That such a man should have such an illness” is said twice: grief that such a person should suffer such a disease, laid at the door of fate.

白Plain Chinese

伯牛病重，孔子去探望，从窗口握着他的手，说：要失去他了，这是命啊！这样好的人竟得了这样的病！这样好的人竟得了这样的病！

常The common reading

His student was gravely ill: why did Confucius only hold his hand from outside the window?

注Key words in the commentary

“夫，音扶” — “夫” is read fú; “有疾，先儒以为癞也” — earlier scholars took the illness to be leprosy; “礼：病者居北牖下，君视之，则迁于南牖下，使君得以南面视己。时伯牛家以此礼尊孔子，孔子不敢当，故不入其室，而自牖执其手，盖与之永诀也” — by ritual the sick lie under the north window; when the ruler visits, they are moved under the south window so the ruler may face south to see them. Boniu's family honoured Confucius with this rite; he did not dare accept it, so he did not enter the room but held his hand through the window, a final farewell; “命，谓天命” — “ming” means heaven's decree.

说What other commentators say

Hou: “伯牛以德行称，亚于颜、闵。故其将死也，孔子尤痛惜之” — Boniu was known for virtuous conduct, second only to Yan and Min; so when he was about to die, Confucius grieved for him especially.
