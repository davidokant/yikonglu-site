Ba yi (Book 3) · chapter 8The painting comes after the white ground

What this chapter says

If the ground is not smooth, every colour you put on comes out blotchy.

白Plain Chinese

子夏问：“笑得真美啊，眼睛真亮啊，白底子上画出了色彩。”这几句诗是什么意思？孔子说：先有白底，然后画画。子夏说：那么，礼也是在后面的吗？孔子说：能启发我的是卜商啊！现在可以和你谈《诗》了。

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

常The common reading

Paint first, or prime first?

注Key words in the commentary

“此逸诗也” — this is a lost ode; “素，粉地，画之质也；绚，采色，画之饰也” — su is the white ground, the substance of a painting; xuan is colour, its ornament; “后素，后于素也” — houxu means after the white; Kaogongji: “绘画之事后素功” — painting comes after the white work; “礼必以忠信为质” — ritual must have loyalty and trust as its substance; “起，犹发也” — qi means to draw out.

说What other commentators say

Xie Liangzuo: “子贡因论学而知诗，子夏因论诗而知学” — Zigong came to the Odes through learning, Zixia came to learning through the Odes; Yang Shi: “白受采……苟无其质，礼不虚行” — white takes colour; without substance, ritual does not work empty — and “玩心于章句之末，则其为诗也固而已矣” — fiddling with phrasing makes one's reading of the Odes rigid.

Compared with Zhu Xi

CommentaryPointRelationConfidenceNote
钱穆《论语新解》绘画先后differsfairly sureQian: first the five colours, then outlining in white; Zhu: first a white ground, then colours. Both make loyalty and good faith the substance for learning ritual; the painting difference should not be read as opposite views of ritual.
杨伯峻《论语译注》礼后word sensesettledYang's translation supplies “ren and rightness first”, Zhu makes loyalty and good faith the substance; both take plain ground first, colour after; the difference lies in naming the substance, not in reversing the painting order.
