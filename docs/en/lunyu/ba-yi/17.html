Ba yi (Book 3) · chapter 17The sheep for announcing the new moon

常The common reading

If only the outward form is left, why keep it?

注Key words in the commentary

gao read gu in Zhu Xi's note, gao today; xi read xi; “饩，生牲也。鲁自文公始不视朔，而有司犹供此羊” — xi is a live victim; from Duke Wen, Lu's rulers stopped attending the new-moon rite, yet officials still supplied the sheep; “爱，犹惜也。子贡盖惜其无实而妄费” — ai means to begrudge; Zigong begrudged the waste on an empty form; “然礼虽废，羊存，犹得以识之而可复焉。若并去其羊，则此礼遂亡矣” — though the rite had lapsed, with the sheep kept it could still be recognised and restored; remove the sheep and the rite is gone.

家What other commentators say

Yang Shi: “羊存，则告朔之名未泯，而其实因可举” — while the sheep remains, the name of the rite survives, and its substance can be revived.

读How Yikonglu reads it

The sheep marks the rite; keep the marker and the rite can still be recognised and restored. Today: an old shop that makes one pot of the old recipe at the winter solstice, not for sale.
