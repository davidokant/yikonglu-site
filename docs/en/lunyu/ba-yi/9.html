Ba yi (Book 3) · chapter 9Not enough records

What this chapter says

Confucius could tell it, but would not rest on his word alone. Today: grandmother's recipe — what's missing is the person standing at the stove.

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：夏朝的礼，我能讲，可它的后代杞国，不足以作证；殷朝的礼，我能讲，可它的后代宋国，不足以作证。这是书和懂行的人都不够的缘故。要是够，我就能拿来作证了。

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

注Key words in the commentary

“杞，夏之后；宋，殷之后” — Qi descends from Xia, Song from Yin; “征，证也” — zheng means to verify; “文，典籍也；献，贤也” — wen is written records, xian is worthy men.

CommentaryPointRelationConfidenceNote
杨伯峻《论语译注》samesettled文 means historical records, 献 worthy men; Yang explicitly follows Zhu, so it should not be read in the modern sense of documents only.
钱穆《论语新解》samefairly sureBoth: 文 records, 献 worthy men, 征 to verify; Qian adds that Confucius could integrate history but the records were too few to prove it; Zhu here only says the evidence is lacking.
