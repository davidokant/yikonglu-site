Ba yi (Book 3) · chapter 4Lin Fang asks about the root of ritual

白Plain Chinese

林放问礼的根本。孔子说：你这个问题意义重大啊！礼，与其奢侈，宁可俭朴；丧事，与其仪节周全，宁可内心悲痛。

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

常The common reading

The simpler the better.

注Key words in the commentary

“易，治也” — yi means arranged, as in tending fields; “节文习熟，而无哀痛惨怛之实” — the forms practised smoothly with no real grief; “礼贵得中……二者皆未合礼” — ritual values the mean, and both extremes fall short; “必先有质而后有文，则质乃礼之本也” — substance must come before form, so substance is the root of ritual.

家What other commentators say

Fan Zuyu: “祭与其敬不足而礼有余也，不若礼不足而敬有余也” — in sacrifice, better too little ceremony and enough reverence than the reverse; Yang Shi: “礼始诸饮食，故汙尊而抔饮” — ritual began with eating and drinking, with hollows for cups and hands for drinking; “周衰，世方以文灭质” — as Zhou declined, form was smothering substance.

读How Yikonglu reads it

When both sides are off, lean towards the root. Today: a table full of dishes vs. a bowl of noodles.
