Ba yi (Book 3) · chapter 16In archery, piercing the hide is not the point

What this chapter says

Yikonglu's reading: The contest is in what can be trained, not in what you were born with. Zhu Xi's commentary: “射不主皮，乡射礼文; 为力不同科，孔子解礼之意” — “piercing the hide is not the point” is from the village archery rite; “strength not of the same grade” is Confucius explaining it; “皮，革也……所谓鹄也” — pi is the leather target; “科，等也” — ke means grade.

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：射箭不以射穿皮靶为主，因为各人的力气不在一个等级上。这是古时的规矩。

注Key words in the commentary

“射不主皮，乡射礼文；为力不同科，孔子解礼之意” — “piercing the hide is not the point” is from the village archery rite; “strength not of the same grade” is Confucius explaining it; “皮，革也……所谓鹄也” — pi is the leather target; “科，等也” — ke means grade; “古者射以观德，但主于中，而不主于贯革” — in old times archery showed virtue, aiming to hit, not to pierce.

说What other commentators say

Book of Rites: “武王克商，散军郊射，而贯革之射息” — after King Wu conquered Shang, armies disbanded and piercing archery ceased; “周衰……复尚贯革，故孔子叹之” — as Zhou declined, piercing was prized again, so Confucius sighed; Yang Shi: “中可以学而能，力不可以强而至” — hitting can be learned; strength cannot be forced.

Compared with Zhu Xi

CommentaryPointRelationConfidenceNote
杨伯峻《论语译注》samesettledRitual archery values hitting the target, not piercing the hide, since strength differs; Yang separates ritual from martial archery, and Zhu also explains it as observing virtue, not prizing strength.
钱穆《论语新解》samefairly sureBoth: archery is about hitting, not piercing, because strength differs. Qian explicitly agrees with Zhu and rejects philological readings that split the three lines or force 主皮 apart from 贯革.
