Ba yi (Book 3) · chapter 3A person without humaneness — what of ritual?

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：一个人没有仁心，礼对他有什么用？一个人没有仁心，乐对他有什么用？

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

注Key words in the commentary

You Zuo: “人心亡矣……礼乐不为之用也” — when the heart is lost, ritual and music do not serve him; Cheng Yi: “仁者天下之正理，失正理则无序而不和” — humaneness is the right principle of the world; lose it and there is neither order nor harmony; Li Yu: “礼乐待人而后行” — ritual and music depend on people to be carried out.

家What other commentators say

“记者序此于八佾雍彻之后，疑其为僭礼乐者发也” — the compilers placed this after the eight rows and the Yong, perhaps aimed at those usurping rites and music (the arrangement is a clue).

读How Yikonglu reads it

Fill the eight rows, sing the emperor's ode — if the person is wrong, ritual and music can do nothing with him.
