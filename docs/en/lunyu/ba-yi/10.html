Ba yi (Book 3) · chapter 10The di sacrifice after the libation

注Key words in the commentary

Zhao Boxun: “禘，王者之大祭也” — di is the king's great sacrifice; “成王以周公有大勋劳，赐鲁重祭……然非礼矣” — King Cheng granted Lu this great sacrifice for the Duke of Zhou's merit, yet it was not proper; “灌者……以降神也” — the libation brings down the spirits; “诚意未散，犹有可观；自此以后，则浸以懈怠” — while sincerity lasts there is something to watch; after that it slackens.

家What other commentators say

Xie Liangzuo links it to the previous chapter: “考之杞宋已如彼，考之当今又如此” — look to Qi and Song and it is like that; look to the present and it is like this.

读How Yikonglu reads it

Looking back, the records and the people are gone; looking around, the rite is there but the heart is not.
