Ba yi (Book 3) · chapter 10The di sacrifice after the libation

What this chapter says

Yikonglu's reading: Looking back, the records and the people are gone; looking around, the rite is there but the heart is not. Zhu Xi's commentary: Zhao Boxun: “禘，王者之大祭也” — di is the king's great sacrifice; “成王以周公有大勋劳，赐鲁重祭……然非礼矣” — King Cheng granted Lu this great sacrifice for the Duke of Zhou's merit, yet it was not proper.

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：禘祭从灌酒请神以后，我就不想再看下去了。

注Key words in the commentary

Zhao Boxun: “禘，王者之大祭也” — di is the king's great sacrifice; “成王以周公有大勋劳，赐鲁重祭……然非礼矣” — King Cheng granted Lu this great sacrifice for the Duke of Zhou's merit, yet it was not proper; “灌者……以降神也” — the libation brings down the spirits; “诚意未散，犹有可观；自此以后，则浸以懈怠” — while sincerity lasts there is something to watch; after that it slackens.

说What other commentators say

Xie Liangzuo links it to the previous chapter: “考之杞宋已如彼，考之当今又如此” — look to Qi and Song and it is like that; look to the present and it is like this.

Compared with Zhu Xi

CommentaryPointRelationConfidenceNote
钱穆《论语新解》differsuncertainDifferent on the di and on why he did not wish to watch: Qian takes the faults of a joint sacrifice and reversed order; Zhu takes the king's sacrifice to the founder's origin, usurped by Lu, sincerity fading after the libation. Qian says he picks one view, not a final verdict.
杨伯峻《论语译注》灌word sensesettledYang: the first offering of wine to the impersonator of the dead; Zhu: pouring fragrant wine on the ground to bring down the spirits. Both set it against Lu's usurped di sacrifice; the rite itself is explained differently.
