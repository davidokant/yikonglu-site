Ba yi (Book 3) · chapter 2The three families clear away to the Yong

白Plain Chinese

孟孙、叔孙、季孙三家祭祖，撤祭品时唱《雍》。孔子说：“诸侯们来助祭，天子仪容庄严”，这两句诗，用在三家的庙堂上，取的是什么意思呢？

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

注Key words in the commentary

“彻，祭毕而收其俎也” — che is clearing the vessels after the sacrifice; “天子宗庙之祭，则歌雍以彻” — at the Son of Heaven's ancestral sacrifices the Yong was sung while clearing; “相，助也；辟公，诸侯也；穆穆，深远之意” — xiang means assist, bigong the lords, mumu deep and grave; “讥其无知妄作，以取僭窃之罪” — mocking them for acting in ignorance and incurring the guilt of usurpation.

家What other commentators say

Cheng Yi traces it to the source: “成王之赐，伯禽之受，皆非也。其因袭之弊，遂使季氏僭八佾，三家僭雍彻” — King Cheng's grant and Boqin's acceptance were both wrong; that inherited fault led the Ji to usurp the eight rows and the three families the Yong.

读How Yikonglu reads it

They didn't even understand the song they sang. Today: a corner shop's banner “Welcoming heads of state”.
