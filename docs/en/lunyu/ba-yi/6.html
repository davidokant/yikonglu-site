Ba yi (Book 3) · chapter 6The Ji sacrifice to Mount Tai

What this chapter says

It was the Ji who needed saving, not Mount Tai. Today: working for a boss you cannot talk out of it.

白Plain Chinese

季氏要去祭泰山。孔子对冉有说：你不能劝阻他吗？冉有说：不能。孔子说：唉！难道泰山的神，还不如林放懂礼吗？

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

注Key words in the commentary

“诸侯祭封内山川，季氏祭之，僭也” — lords sacrifice to the mountains and rivers within their realm; for the Ji to do so was usurpation; Ran You “时为季氏宰” — was then steward of the Ji; “救，谓救其陷于僭窃之罪” — save means save them from falling into the guilt of usurpation; “神不享非礼” — spirits do not accept improper offerings.

说What other commentators say

Fan Zuyu: “圣人不轻绝人。尽己之心，安知冉有之不能救，季氏之不可谏也” — the sage does not lightly give up on people; doing his utmost, how could he know Ran You could not save them or the Ji could not be admonished?

Compared with Zhu Xi

CommentaryPointRelationConfidenceNote
杨伯峻《论语译注》samesettled旅 is a sacrifice to mountains; the Ji family sacrificing to Mount Tai usurped ritual, and Ran You should have stopped it. Zhu adds that Lin Fang is invoked to spur Ran You; Yang stresses rank and the right to sacrifice to mountains.
钱穆《论语新解》samefairly sureBoth: 旅 is a sacrifice, 救 is saving the Ji from usurping ritual; Mount Tai will not accept an improper rite. Qian argues that if there are spirits, there must be ritual, without presenting the spirits' existence as known.
