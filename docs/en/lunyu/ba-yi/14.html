Ba yi (Book 3) · chapter 14Zhou looked to the two dynasties

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：周朝的礼，借鉴了夏、商两代，多么丰富多彩啊！我跟从周朝。

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

注Key words in the commentary

“监，视也” — jian means to look at; “言其视二代之礼而损益之” — it studied the rites of the two dynasties and took away and added; “郁郁，文盛貌” — yuyu means rich in culture.

家What other commentators say

Yin Tun: “三代之礼，至周大备” — the rites of the three dynasties were complete with Zhou.

读How Yikonglu reads it

Following Zhou means following the work of improving on predecessors bit by bit. Today: the dishes at New Year's Eve dinner.
