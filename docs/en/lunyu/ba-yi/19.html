Ba yi (Book 3) · chapter 19The lord employs his ministers with ritual

注Key words in the commentary

“定公，鲁君，名宋” — Duke Ding, ruler of Lu, named Song; “二者皆理之当然，各欲自尽而已” — both are what is right, and each should simply do his own part fully.

家What other commentators say

Lü Dalin: “使臣不患其不忠，患礼之不至；事君不患其无礼，患忠之不足” — a lord should worry not that ministers are disloyal but that his own courtesy falls short; a minister should worry not that the lord is discourteous but that his own loyalty falls short; Yin Tun: “君臣以义合者也。故君使臣以礼，则臣事君以忠” — lord and minister are joined by what is right, so courtesy from above brings loyalty from below.

读How Yikonglu reads it

Each side looks only at its own end; Duke Ding was the ruler, and his question was answered with his own half. Today: a team lead first asks whether people are treated with enough respect; the team member first asks whether the work is done properly.
