Ba yi (Book 3) · chapter 24The border warden of Yi asks to see him

What this chapter says

Yikonglu's reading: A minor official saw Confucius's weight at first sight. Today: an old teacher moved off the podium runs free classes in the community, and more people come than were ever in his class. A common question is “Losing office turned out to be a good thing” — the line above is Yikonglu's answer.

白Plain Chinese

仪地管边界的小官请求见孔子，说：凡是到过这里的君子，我没有见不到的。孔子的随行学生让他见了。他出来以后说：诸位何必担心老师丢了官呢？天下无道已经很久了，上天要把你们的老师当作木铎。

常The common reading

Losing office turned out to be a good thing?

注Key words in the commentary

xian (be presented) read xian, sang (lose) in the departing tone; “仪，卫邑。封人，掌封疆之官，盖贤而隐于下位者也” — Yi was a town of Wei; the border warden kept the frontier, probably a worthy man hidden in a low post; “木铎，金口木舌，施政教时所振，以警众者也” — the muduo is a bell with a metal mouth and wooden clapper, shaken when proclaiming government and teaching, to alert the crowd; “封人一见夫子而遽以是称之，其所得于观感之间者深矣” — that the warden, on seeing the Master once, at once called him this shows how deeply he perceived him.

说What other commentators say

Zhu Xi records two readings side by side: ① “乱极当治，天必将使夫子得位设教，不久失位也” — disorder at its height must turn to order; Heaven will surely give the Master a post to teach, and he will not be out of office long; ② another view, “天使夫子失位，周流四方以行其教，如木铎之徇于道路也” — Heaven made the Master lose office so he would travel the four quarters spreading his teaching, as the bell is carried along the roads. Zhu Xi does not choose for the reader.

杨伯峻《论语译注》samesettled丧 in the departing tone: losing office; the wooden clapper stands for teaching from office, Yang renders it “teacher of the people”. Zhu also keeps the reading of wandering and teaching after losing office, so Zhu does not have only one reading.
