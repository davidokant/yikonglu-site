Ba yi (Book 3) · chapter 23The Master speaks of music to the Grand Music Master of Lu

What this chapter says

Yikonglu's reading: First together, then harmonious, each part clear within the harmony, linked to the end. Today: a choir starts together, then blends, each voice part still audible, singing through in one breath.

白Plain Chinese

孔子告诉鲁国的乐官怎样奏乐，说：音乐是可以懂得的：开始演奏，各种乐器合在一起；接着放开，声音和谐，节奏分明，连绵不断，一直到一曲终了。

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

注Key words in the commentary

yu read yù, da read tai, cong read zong in Zhu Xi's note and cong today; “时音乐废缺，故孔子教之” — music had fallen into disrepair, so Confucius taught him; “翕，合也。从，放也。纯，和也。皦，明也。绎，相续不绝也。成，乐之一终也” — xi is coming together, cong unfolding, chun harmonious, jiao clear, yi continuing unbroken, cheng the completion of one movement.

说What other commentators say

Xie Liangzuo: “五音合矣，清浊高下，如五味之相济而后和” — the five notes come together, high and low, light and heavy, like the five flavours balancing into harmony; “合而和矣，欲其无相夺伦” — together and harmonious, without crowding one another out; “不相反而相连，如贯珠可也” — not opposed but linked, like pearls on a string.

Compared with Zhu Xi

CommentaryPointRelationConfidenceNote
杨伯峻《论语译注》samefairly sureMusic moves from playing together to harmony, clarity and continuity, ending complete; Zhu glosses 翕 as joining and 从 as unfolding; Yang's “ardent” opening describes the start, not a different piece.
钱穆《论语新解》samefairly sureBoth read purity, brightness and continuity as the course of the music. Qian links the ardent opening to drums and bells and the stirring of listeners, Zhu explains 翕 as the five notes joining: a different focus at the start.
