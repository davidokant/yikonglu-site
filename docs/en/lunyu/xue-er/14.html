Xue er (Book 1) · chapter 14A gentleman does not seek to eat his fill

注Key words in the commentary

“志有在而不暇及也” — his mind is elsewhere and he has no time for it; “敏于事者，勉其所不足” — quick in action means pushing on where one falls short; “必就有道之人，以正其是非” — one must go to someone who has the Way to have one's right and wrong set straight.

家What other commentators say

Yin Tun cites Yang Zhu and Mozi as “学仁义而差者也” — people who studied ren and yi and went astray.

读How Yikonglu reads it

Not going hungry on purpose; simply too occupied to mind.

杨伯峻《论语译注》Zhonghua Book Co. · modern, short quote

“君子，吃食不要求饱足，居住不要求舒适，对工作勤劳敏捷，说话却谨慎，到有道的人那里去匡正自己，这样，可以说是好学了。”

钱穆《论语新解》modern, short quote

“君子，饮食不求饱，居处不求安，敏疾地做事，谨慎地说话，又能常向有道之人来辨正自己的是非，这样可算是好学了。”

Compared with Zhu Xi

CommentaryPointRelationNote
杨伯峻《论语译注》正word sense杨取匡正自己，并不取判断是非得失；朱为就有道者正其是非。
钱穆《论语新解》same志在学而不求安饱，又就有道者辨正是非。
