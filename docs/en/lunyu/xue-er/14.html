Xue er (Book 1) · chapter 14A gentleman does not seek to eat his fill

What this chapter says

Yikonglu's reading: Not going hungry on purpose; simply too occupied to mind. Zhu Xi's commentary: “志有在而不暇及也” — his mind is elsewhere and he has no time for it; “敏于事者，勉其所不足” — quick in action means pushing on where one falls short; “必就有道之人，以正其是非” — one must go to someone who has the Way to have one's right and wrong set straight.

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：君子吃饭不求饱，居住不求安逸，做事勤快、说话谨慎，到有道的人那里去端正自己的是非，可以说是好学了。
