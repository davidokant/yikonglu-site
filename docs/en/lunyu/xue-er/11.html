Xue er (Book 1) · chapter 11While the father lives, observe the son's intent

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：父亲在世时，看儿子的志向；父亲去世后，看儿子的行为；三年里不急着改父亲的做法，可以算孝了。

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

常The common reading

Even if the father was wrong, change nothing for three years.

注Key words in the commentary

“父在，子不得自专，而志则可知；父没，然后其行可见” — while the father lives the son cannot act on his own, but his intent can be known; after the father's death his conduct can be seen.

家What other commentators say

Yin Tun: “如其道，虽终身无改可也；如其非道，何待三年” — if the way was right, never change it; if it was wrong, why wait three years? You Zuo: it refers to “在所当改而可以未改者耳” — things that ought to be changed but can wait.

读How Yikonglu reads it

What is wrong, change now; what can wait, let wait — that is time given to what one cannot bear to let go. Today: the small factory a father left behind.
