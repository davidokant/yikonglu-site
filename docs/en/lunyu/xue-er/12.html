Xue er (Book 1) · chapter 12In the use of ritual, harmony is prized

What this chapter says

Yikonglu's reading: Harmony alone, not regulated by ritual, does not work either. Today: in a team where no one wants to offend anyone, every meeting is all “looks good” and “fine by me”; the meetings are pleasant, and the work drifts further into a mess. The common view is “Harmony above all; never argue”; Yikonglu's reading puts the weight elsewhere.

白Plain Chinese

有子说：礼的运用，以从容不迫为可贵。古代圣王的道，好就好在这里，小事大事都照着做。但也有行不通的时候：只知道求和而一味地和，不用礼来节制，也是行不通的。

常The common reading

Harmony above all; never argue.

注Key words in the commentary

“礼者，天理之节文，人事之仪则也” — ritual is the measured pattern of heavenly principle and the rule of human affairs; “和者，从容不迫之意” — he is composure without strain, not “being agreeable”; Zhu Xi: “严而泰，和而节” — strict yet at ease, harmonious yet measured.

说What other commentators say

Cheng Yi: “礼胜则离，乐胜则流” — too much ritual divides, too much music dissolves; Fan Zuyu: “礼之体主于敬，其用以和为贵” — the substance of ritual is reverence, its use prizes harmony.
