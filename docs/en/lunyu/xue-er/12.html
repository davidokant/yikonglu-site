Xue er (Book 1) · chapter 12In the use of ritual, harmony is prized

常The common reading

Harmony above all; never argue.

注Key words in the commentary

“礼者，天理之节文，人事之仪则也” — ritual is the measured pattern of heavenly principle and the rule of human affairs; “和者，从容不迫之意” — he is composure without strain, not “being agreeable”; Zhu Xi: “严而泰，和而节” — strict yet at ease, harmonious yet measured.

家What other commentators say

Cheng Yi: “礼胜则离，乐胜则流” — too much ritual divides, too much music dissolves; Fan Zuyu: “礼之体主于敬，其用以和为贵” — the substance of ritual is reverence, its use prizes harmony.

读How Yikonglu reads it

Harmony alone, with nothing to regulate it, does not work either. Today: the team where everyone only ever says “looks good”.
