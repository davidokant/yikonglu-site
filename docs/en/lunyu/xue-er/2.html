Xue er (Book 1) · chapter 2Filial and fraternal duty as the root of ren

常The common reading

Filial and fraternal duty is the root of ren (humaneness).

注Key words in the commentary

“为仁，犹曰行仁” — wei ren here means practising ren; Cheng Yi: “谓之行仁之本则可，谓是仁之本则不可” — call it the root of practising ren, yes; the root of ren itself, no; “与者，疑辞，谦退不敢质言也” — the final particle yu marks a question, a modest refusal to assert.

读How Yikonglu reads it

Filial and fraternal duty is where practising ren begins. Today: kind to strangers online, no patience for one's parents at home — that is starting from the far end.
