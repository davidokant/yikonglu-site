Xue er (Book 1) · chapter 1Learning and practising it in season

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：学了，时时拿出来练、拿出来照着做，不也高兴吗？有志同道合的人从远方来，不也快乐吗？别人不了解我，我也不怨恨，不也是君子吗？

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

常The common reading

You learned it, so review it on schedule.

注Key words in the commentary

“学之为言效也” — to learn is to imitate, to do as someone else does; “习，鸟数飞也” — xi is a fledgling beating its wings again and again; Xie Liangzuo: “时习者，无时而不习” — practising in season means there is no moment one is not practising (sitting like a corpse at a sacrifice, standing as if at a fast).

家What other commentators say

Cheng Yi also says “时复思绎”, to go over it in one's mind again and again; so the “review” reading is very old. Zhu Xi shifts the weight from the head to the body.

读How Yikonglu reads it

What you learned, keep taking out to practise and to do. Today: watching the tutorial ten times is watching another bird fly.
