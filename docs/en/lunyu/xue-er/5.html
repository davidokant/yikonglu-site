Xue er (Book 1) · chapter 5Governing a state of a thousand chariots

What this chapter says

Cheng Yi: “此言至浅……若推其极，尧舜之治亦不过此” — the words are plain, but pushed to the limit, even Yao and Shun governed no differently. Today: a shopkeeper who keeps his word, doesn't waste money, and doesn't send staff on private errands in the busy season.

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：治理一个有千辆兵车的国家，办事要专心谨慎并且守信，节省用度并且爱护人，役使百姓要在农闲的时候。

常The common reading

No common misreading; the question is why governing a state should be only these five things.

注Key words in the commentary

“道，治也” — dao here means to govern; “敬者，主一无适之谓” — jing (care) is keeping the mind on one thing and not wandering; “时，谓农隙之时” — the right season is the slack time in farming; Yang Shi cites the Changes: “节以制度，不伤财，不害民” — regulate by set measures, and neither wealth nor people are harmed.
