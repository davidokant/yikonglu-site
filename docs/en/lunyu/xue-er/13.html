Xue er (Book 1) · chapter 13Trust that comes close to what is right

注Key words in the commentary

“信，约信也” — xin here is a pledge; “义者，事之宜也” — yi is what is fitting in the matter; “复，践言也” — fu is to carry out one's word; “皆当谨之于始而虑其所终” — all of these call for care at the start and thought for where they end.

读How Yikonglu reads it

The work happens before you say yes. Today: agreeing at midnight, without a thought, to lend the money.
