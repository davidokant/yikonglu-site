Xue er (Book 1) · chapter 8A gentleman without weight has no dignity

常The common reading

Only befriend people better than yourself (which sounds like social climbing).

注Key words in the commentary

“无、毋通，禁止辞” — wu is a prohibition; “友所以辅仁，不如己，则无益而有损” — friends are for helping one toward ren; one who is not up to you brings no gain and some loss; “惮，畏难也” — dan is to shrink from difficulty.

家What other commentators say

You Zuo strings the four clauses into one line: weight as the base — learning — loyalty and trust — the help of those who surpass you — correcting faults without fear; if you don't correct them, the worthy stop telling you.

读How Yikonglu reads it

Zhu Xi takes the words as they stand and does not talk his way around them. Today: the one person willing to tell you to your face tells you twice; if you don't change, the third time he says nothing.
