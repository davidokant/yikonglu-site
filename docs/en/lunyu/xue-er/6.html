Xue er (Book 1) · chapter 6A young man at home is filial

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：年轻人在家孝顺父母，出门敬爱兄长，做事谨慎、说话守信，广泛地爱众人，亲近有仁德的人。做到这些还有余力，就去学诗书六艺。

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

常The common reading

Study comes last, and is optional.

注Key words in the commentary

“文，谓诗书六艺之文” — wen means the texts of the Odes, the Documents and the six arts; “余力，犹言暇日” — strength left over means spare time.

家What other commentators say

Cheng Yi and Yin Tun stress the root over the branches; Hong Xingzu pulls back with “文灭其质” and “质胜而野” — too much polish drowns the substance, too little leaves it rough; Zhu Xi: without study “无以考圣贤之成法……所行或出于私意” — one cannot check against the sages' established rule, and what one does may come from private whim.

读How Yikonglu reads it

This is about order, not about choosing one and dropping the other. Today: the capable person who never studied has nothing to check himself against when he is wrong.
