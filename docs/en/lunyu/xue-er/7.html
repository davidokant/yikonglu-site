Xue er (Book 1) · chapter 7Honouring the worthy over good looks

常The common reading

Several readings circulate online: valuing virtue over looks; choosing a wife for her virtue.

注Key words in the commentary

“贤人之贤，而易其好色之心，好善有诚也” — to prize a worthy person's worth and exchange one's fondness for beauty for it, is to love goodness sincerely; “致，犹委也……不有其身” — zhi means to hand over, to no longer keep one's person for oneself.

家What other commentators say

Yang Bojun reads the first clause as the husband-wife relation, completing a set of four; Wu Yu criticises Zixia: “其流之弊，将或至于废学” — pushed too far, this leads to abandoning study.

读How Yikonglu reads it

Someone who never studied but does all this — Zixia says he has studied. Today: the elders who had only a few years of schooling.
