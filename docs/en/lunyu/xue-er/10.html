Xue er (Book 1) · chapter 10Whenever the Master arrives in a state

注Key words in the commentary

Each of the five virtues is glossed word by word (“良，易直也” — liang is easy-going and straight); “夫子未尝求之，但其德容如是，故时君敬信，自以其政就而问之耳” — the Master never sought it; his bearing being what it was, the rulers of the day respected and trusted him and came to ask him about their own government.

家What other commentators say

Zhang Shi: everyone wanted to listen, no one was willing to use him — “私欲害之，是以终不能用耳”, private desire got in the way, so in the end they could not.

读How Yikonglu reads it

Coming to ask is not the same as handing you the job.
