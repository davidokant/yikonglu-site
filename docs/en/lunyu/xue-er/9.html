Xue er (Book 1) · chapter 9Care at the end, remembrance of the distant

白Plain Chinese

曾子说：父母去世时慎重地办好丧礼，对久远的祖先诚心祭祀，百姓的德行就会归于厚道。

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

常The common reading

Finish what you start.

注Key words in the commentary

“慎终者，丧尽其礼；追远者，祭尽其诚” — care at the end means the full rites of mourning; remembrance of the distant means full sincerity in sacrifice; “终者人之所易忽，远者人之所易忘……厚之道也” — the end is what people easily neglect, the distant what they easily forget; attending to them is the way of fullness.

读How Yikonglu reads it

Put your heart into the places that are easy to neglect and easy to forget. Today: driving two hundred kilometres to stand ten minutes at a grave.

Compared with Zhu Xi

CommentaryPointRelationNote
钱穆《论语新解》同解而有扩展word sense均以丧祭厚人心；钱另以超越功利的真情说明，并讨论不肯定死后灵魂，不把这些扩展写成经文原义。
杨伯峻《论语译注》same均落在丧祭与风俗归厚；杨译文从字面说死亡、祖先，注中补丧祭之实。
