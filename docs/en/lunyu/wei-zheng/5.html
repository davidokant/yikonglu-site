Wei zheng (Book 2) · chapter 5Meng Yizi asks about filial piety

What this chapter says

Today: borrowing money to throw a lavish birthday banquet is “doing what one may not”.

白Plain Chinese

孟懿子问孝。孔子说：不要违背。樊迟给孔子赶车，孔子告诉他：孟孙问我孝，我回答他“不要违背”。樊迟问：这是什么意思？孔子说：父母活着，按礼侍奉；去世了，按礼安葬，按礼祭祀。

常The common reading

“Do not go against” = do whatever your parents say.

注Key words in the commentary

“无违，谓不背于理” — not going against means not turning one's back on what is right; “礼，即理之节文” — ritual is the measured form of what is right; Confucius explained it to Fan Chi “恐其失指，而以从亲之令为孝” — fearing the point would be missed and obeying a parent's orders taken for filial piety.

说What other commentators say

Hu Yin: “心虽无穷，而分则有限；得为而不为，与不得为而为之，均于不孝” — the heart is boundless but one's station has limits; failing to do what one may, and doing what one may not, are equally unfilial; Zhu Xi notes that the three great families were usurping rites at the time, “以是警之” — this was a warning to them.
