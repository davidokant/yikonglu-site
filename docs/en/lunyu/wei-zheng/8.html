Wei zheng (Book 2) · chapter 8Zixia asks about filial piety (the hard part is the face)

What this chapter says

Yikonglu's reading: All the work was done; the face never arrived. A common question is “Whose face is the hard part” — the line above is Yikonglu's answer. Zhu Xi's commentary: “惟色为难” — only the expression is hard.

白Plain Chinese

子夏问孝。孔子说：难的是脸色。有事，年轻人替父兄去做；有酒饭，请父兄先吃，难道这样就算孝了吗？

常The common reading

Whose face is the hard part?

注Key words in the commentary

“惟色为难” — only the expression is hard; “深爱者必有和气，和气者必有愉色，愉色者必有婉容” — deep love brings a warm air, a warm air brings a glad expression, a glad expression brings a gentle manner; “先生，父兄也” — “elders” means fathers and elder brothers.

说What other commentators say

An older reading: “承顺父母之色为难，亦通” — reading the parents' faces and complying is hard, also possible; Zhu Xi, from what follows, places it on the child's face. Cheng Yi on the four questions about filial piety: “各因其材之高下，与其所失而告之” — each was answered according to his ability and his failing.
