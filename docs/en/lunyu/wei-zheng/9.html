Wei zheng (Book 2) · chapter 9Hui is not slow

What this chapter says

Whether someone has learned shows outside the classroom.

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：我和颜回讲一整天，他从不提反对的意见，像个笨人。他退下以后，我看他私下的言行，也足以把我讲的道理发挥出来。颜回不笨啊。

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

注Key words in the commentary

“不违者，意不相背，有听受而无问难也” — not disagreeing means taking it in without objecting; “私，谓燕居独处” — private means when at leisure and alone; “发，谓发明所言之理” — bring out means to make clear the principle spoken of.

说What other commentators say

Kong Anguo (in the Collected Explanations) takes it as Yan Hui explaining to fellow students; Zhu Xi quotes his teacher Li Tong: “默识心融，触处洞然” — silently grasped and dissolved into the heart, clear wherever it touches.
