Wei zheng (Book 2) · chapter 2Thoughts without crookedness

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：《诗》三百篇，用一句话来概括，就是：让人的思想不走邪路。

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

常The common reading

All three hundred Odes are pure and innocent.

注Key words in the commentary

“蔽，犹盖也” — bi means to cover; “凡诗之言，善者可以感发人之善心，恶者可以惩创人之逸志” — in the Odes, the good lines stir a reader's good heart, and the bad ones chasten a reader's wayward will.

家What other commentators say

Cheng Yi: “思无邪者，诚也” — thoughts without crookedness means sincerity; Fan Zuyu: “知要则能守约” — knowing the essential, one can hold to what is concise.

读How Yikonglu reads it

The “without crookedness” lies in the reader, not entirely in the poems. Today: a novel about a villain can still bring a reader round.
