The Analects › Wei zheng (Book 2)

Wei zheng (Book 2) · chapter 3Guiding them by decrees

常The common reading

Confucius was against laws and punishments.

注Key words in the commentary

“道犹引导，谓先之也” — dao means to lead, to go first; “政谓法制禁令” — zheng means laws, regulations and prohibitions; “苟免刑罚而无所羞愧……为恶之心未尝忘也” — merely escaping punishment without shame, the wish to do wrong is never gone; “格，至也” — ge means to arrive (another reading: to be set right).

家What other commentators say

Zhu Xi: “政者为治之具，刑者辅治之法，德礼则所以出治之本……虽不可以偏废” — decrees are the tools of government, punishments its auxiliary, virtue and ritual the root from which government issues; none can be dropped.

读How Yikonglu reads it

Punishment and decree keep people from crime; virtue and ritual move them toward good. Today: the camera breaks, and the rubbish comes back.
