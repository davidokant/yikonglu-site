Wei zheng (Book 2) · chapter 23Can ten generations be known?

What this chapter says

Yikonglu's reading: He watched two handovers and saw what stayed and what changed. The common view is “Confucius could foretell the future”; Yikonglu's reading puts the weight elsewhere.

白Plain Chinese

子张问：十代以后的事，能预先知道吗？孔子说：殷继承夏的礼，增减了什么，是可以知道的；周继承殷的礼，增减了什么，也是可以知道的。将来如果有继承周的，就是一百代以后，也是可以知道的。

Close reading and checking

常The common reading

Confucius could foretell the future.

注Key words in the commentary

“王者易姓受命为一世” — each change of ruling house receiving the mandate is one generation; “也，一作乎” — ye is written hu in one version (Lu Deming).

说What other commentators say

Ma Rong: “所因，谓三纲五常；所损益，谓文质三统” — what is followed is the three bonds and five constants, what is changed is form and substance and the three sequences (adopted by Zhu Xi, not Confucius's own words); Hu Yin: “因往推来” — inferring the future from the past; Zhu Xi: “非若后世谶纬术数之学也” — not like the prophecy and numerology of later ages.

CommentaryPointRelationConfidenceNote
钱穆《论语新解》differsfairly sureQian takes a 世 as thirty years, ten 世 as three hundred; Zhu takes a 世 as one dynasty receiving the mandate. Close in the main idea that later ages can be foreseen.
杨伯峻《论语译注》samefairly sureBoth infer the future from what history keeps and changes; Yang sets no thirty-year reading of 世, so his “ten generations” should not be equated with Qian's.
