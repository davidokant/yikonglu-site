Wei zheng (Book 2) · chapter 21Why do you not take part in government?

What this chapter says

Yikonglu's reading: The words are a pretext, and the point is also true. Zhu Xi's commentary: “定公初年，孔子不仕” — in the early years of Duke Ding, Confucius did not serve; “书，周书君陈篇” — the Documents here is the Jun Chen chapter of the Zhou Documents; “善兄弟曰友” — being good to one's brothers is called you.

白Plain Chinese

有人对孔子说：您为什么不从政呢？孔子说：《尚书》上说，孝啊！只要孝顺父母、友爱兄弟，再把这种风气推到政事上。这也就是从政了，为什么一定要做官才算从政呢？

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

注Key words in the commentary

“定公初年，孔子不仕” — in the early years of Duke Ding, Confucius did not serve; “书，周书君陈篇” — the Documents here is the Jun Chen chapter of the Zhou Documents; “善兄弟曰友” — being good to one's brothers is called you; “推广此心，以为一家之政” — extending this heart makes the government of a household.

说What other commentators say

The punctuation of the quotation has always varied; Zhu Xi: “盖孔子之不仕，有难以语或人者，故托此以告之” — Confucius's reasons for not serving were hard to tell this person, so he answered with this — and also “要之至理亦不外是” — yet the deepest principle is no other than this.
