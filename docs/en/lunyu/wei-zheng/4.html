The Analects › Wei zheng (Book 2)

Wei zheng (Book 2) · chapter 4At fifteen I set my heart on learning

常The common reading

A life timetable arranged by age.

注Key words in the commentary

“心之所之谓之志” — zhi (resolve) is where the heart goes; “于事物之所当然，皆无所疑” — no doubt about what things ought to be (no doubts); “天命……事物所以当然之故” — Heaven's decree is the reason why things ought to be so; “矩，法度之器” — ju is the carpenter's square, the instrument of measure.

家What other commentators say

Cheng Yi: “圣人未必然，但为学者立法，使之盈科而后进” — the sage was not necessarily like this himself; he sets out a rule for learners, so that they fill each hollow before moving on; Zhu Xi's own note: “因其近似以自名” — he named the stages by what they resembled.

读How Yikonglu reads it

Not a certificate issued automatically at a certain age, but a road of learning.

杨伯峻《论语译注》Zhonghua Book Co. · modern, short quote

“我十五岁，有志于学问；三十岁，〔懂礼仪，〕说话做事都有把握；四十岁，〔掌握了各种知识，〕不致迷惑；五十岁，得知天命；六十岁，一听别人言语，便可以分别真假，判明是非；到了七十岁，便随心所欲，任何念头不越出规矩。”

钱穆《论语新解》modern, short quote

“我十五岁时，始有志于学。”

CommentaryPointRelationNote
杨伯峻《论语译注》耳顺word sense杨暂译辨言语真假是非；朱为声入心通、知之极至。杨自标姑取一解。
钱穆《论语新解》解释重心不同word sense钱由守道受困引出知天命，又由通达正反顺逆解释耳顺；朱从天道赋物、穷理尽性与知之至论述。
