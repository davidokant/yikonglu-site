Wei zheng (Book 2) · chapter 16Working at strange doctrines

What this chapter says

Following Zhu Xi: burrowing into it is what does harm.

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：专门去钻研偏离正道的学说，这就有害了。（照朱熹所引范祖禹、程子的读法。）

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

常The common reading

Attack heresies.

注Key words in the commentary

Fan Zuyu: “攻，专治也，故治木石金玉之工曰攻” — gong means working at something intently, as craftsmen work wood, stone, metal and jade; “异端，非圣人之道，而别为一端，如杨墨是也” — strange doctrines are not the sages' way but a separate end, like Yang Zhu and Mozi.

说What other commentators say

Cheng Yi held Buddhism to be the greater harm (a Song judgement about its own day); Liu Baonan's Correct Meaning records Sun Yi: gong = attack, yi = stop — the opposite meaning.

CommentaryPointRelationConfidenceNote
钱穆《论语新解》differsfairly sureQian takes 攻 as specialising and 异端 as the opposite extreme, warning against one-sidedness; Zhu takes 异端 as what is not the sages' Way, citing Yang Zhu and Mozi.
杨伯峻《论语译注》攻word sensefairly sureYang: 攻 is attacking and 已 is wiping out; Zhu cites Fan taking 攻 as devoting oneself to, so devoting oneself to heterodoxy is harmful.
