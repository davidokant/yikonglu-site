Wei zheng (Book 2) · chapter 11Reviewing the old to know the new

What this chapter says

If you can only recite it, reheating it a hundred times still gives the same bowl.

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：温习旧有的知识，每次都能有新的体会，就可以做老师了。

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

注Key words in the commentary

“温，寻绎也” — wen means drawing out a thread; “故者，旧所闻；新者，今所得” — the old is what one heard before, the new is what one gains now; “记问之学，则无得于心” — learning by memorising gains nothing in the heart.

说What other commentators say

Zheng Xuan reads wen as reheating cold meat.
