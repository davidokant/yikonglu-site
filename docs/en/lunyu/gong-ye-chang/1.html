公冶长第五 · chapter 1Gongye Chang is fit to be given a wife

白Plain Chinese

孔子评说公冶长：女儿可以嫁给他。他虽然坐过牢，罪却不在他。于是把自己的女儿许配给他。孔子评说南容：天下有道时，他总会被任用；天下无道时，他也能免遭刑罚杀戮。于是把哥哥的女儿许配给他。

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

常The common reading

Confucius married his daughter to a man who had been in prison: read as “he did not mind” or “he was broad-minded”.

注Key words in the commentary

Qi (妻) in the falling tone: to give a daughter in marriage. “缧，黑索也。绁，挛也” — lei is a black rope, xie is a binding; “长之为人无所考，而夫子称其可妻，其必有以取之矣” — nothing is known of Chang's character, but since the Master called him fit to marry, there must have been something to recommend him; “夫有罪无罪，在我而已，岂以自外至者为荣辱哉” — guilt or innocence lies in oneself alone; how could what comes from outside be one's honour or shame? On Nan Rong: “不废，言必见用也……以其谨于言行” — not cast aside means he will surely be employed, because he was careful in word and deed.

家What other commentators say

The Cheng brothers reject the “avoiding suspicion” reading: “此以己之私心窥圣人也。凡人避嫌者，皆内不足也……嫁女必量其才而求配” — this is to peer at the sage with one's own selfish mind; all who avoid suspicion are lacking within; in marrying a daughter one must weigh a man's ability and seek a match.

读How Yikonglu reads it

What matters is not that Confucius was tolerant, but the one judgement “was the crime his or not”: a prison imposed from outside is not his honour or shame. The Nan Rong section says that care in word and deed stands firm in good times and bad. Today: someone once wrongly disciplined and later cleared — some people avoid him, others keep handing him the important work.
