公冶长第五 · chapter 15Zichan had four ways of the gentleman

白Plain Chinese

孔子评说子产：他身上有四样君子之道：待己谦逊，奉上谨慎，养民给人实惠，差使百姓合乎事理。

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

常The common reading

Read as Confucius praising Zichan point by point, showing Zichan was perfect.

注Key words in the commentary

“恭，谦逊也。敬，谨恪也。惠，爱利也” — gong is modesty, jing is carefulness, hui is loving benefit; “使民义，如都鄙有章，上下有服，田有封洫，庐井有伍之类” — employing the people justly means such things as capital and outlying districts having their rules, upper and lower having their garments, fields having boundaries and ditches, and households being grouped in fives.

家What other commentators say

Wu Yu: “数其事而责之者，其所善者多也……数其事而称之者，犹有所未至也……今或以一言盖一人，一事盖一时，皆非也” — when one lists someone's deeds to blame him, his good points are many; when one lists his deeds to praise him, there is still something he has not reached; now to sum up a person in one phrase, or an age in one event, is always wrong.

读How Yikonglu reads it

Praising point by point actually shows there is still somewhere he has not reached. Respectful to himself, reverent to superiors, kind to the people, just in employing them: each of the four has its own object. Judging a person by one phrase or one event is wrong. Today: don't judge a small shop open for thirty years by one bad review.
