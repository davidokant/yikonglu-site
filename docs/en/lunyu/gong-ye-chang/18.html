公冶长第五 · chapter 18Not even this counts as ren?

What this chapter says

Loyalty and purity are single virtues; ren asks whether one fully accords with principle and truly has no selfish mind. Trusting small things to vouch for great ones was Zizhang's fault. Today: leaving a job with a clean handover, even the password book left behind, is loyalty to duty — no more than that yet.

白Plain Chinese

子张问：楚国令尹子文三回出任令尹，不见他面露喜色；三回被免去，也不见他面带恼意；卸任时，总把自己任内的政务原原本本告诉接任的人。这人如何？孔子说：是忠。子张问：够得上仁吗？孔子说：说不准，哪里就谈得上仁？子张又问：崔杼杀了齐庄公，陈文子家有马四十匹，全数舍下，离齐而去；到了另一国，说：“这里当政的，和我们那位崔大夫一样。”又离开；再到一国，又说：“这里当政的，也和崔大夫一样。”又离开。这人如何？孔子说：是清。子张问：够得上仁吗？孔子说：说不准，哪里就谈得上仁？

常The common reading

Taking office three times without joy, dismissed three times without resentment, and abandoning his fortune to leave a disordered state — how could that not be ren?

注Key words in the commentary

“三已之” — “yi” means to be dismissed; “十乘，四十匹” — ten teams are forty horses; “违，去也” — “wei” means to leave; in “未知，焉得仁” the “知” keeps its ordinary reading, to know.

说What other commentators say

Zhu Xi: of Ziwen, “但许其忠，而未许其仁” — he granted only his loyalty, not his ren; of Wenzi, “特许其清，而不许其仁” — he granted only his purity, not his ren. He cites his teacher: “当理而无私心，则仁矣” — to accord with principle with no selfish mind, that is ren; “子张未识仁体，而悦于苟难，遂以小者信其大者” — Zizhang did not yet know the substance of ren and was taken with hard feats, so he trusted the small to vouch for the great; “以他书考之” — checking other books, Ziwen “所谋者无非僭王猾夏之事” — all he planned was usurping the royal title and troubling the central states, and Wenzi “不数岁而复反于齐” — within a few years went back to Qi.
