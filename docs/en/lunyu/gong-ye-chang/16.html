公冶长第五 · chapter 16Is it more polite not to stand on ceremony with old friends?

What this chapter says

Respect fading with time is the usual course; keeping it is what “good at associating with others” means. Today: after five years sharing a flat, a roommate still knocks before borrowing your charger.

白Plain Chinese

孔子说：晏平仲懂得与人相处，相交日久，依旧待人恭敬。

常The common reading

Once you have known someone a long time you no longer need formalities; being polite only keeps them at a distance.

注Key words in the commentary

“久而敬之” — long acquainted, still respectful.

说What other commentators say

Zhu Xi: “晏平仲，齐大夫，名婴” — Yan Pingzhong, a grandee of Qi, personal name Ying. He cites Master Cheng: “人交久则敬衰，久而能敬，所以为善” — when people have known each other long, respect fades; to keep respect after a long time is what makes him good at it.
