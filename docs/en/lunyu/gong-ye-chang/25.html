公冶长第五 · chapter 25Why was Confucius' wish greater than Zilu's and Yan Yuan's?

What this chapter says

Yikonglu's reading: All three share with others, differing in scale: Zilu shares carriage and horses with friends, Yan Yuan does not boast or show off, Confucius lets each find their place, like heaven and earth nurturing without a trace. Today: the auntie in the housing block whom the elderly trust with their keys and to whose door the children run first after school. A common question is “Why is Confucius' wish greater than those of Zilu and Yan Yuan” — the line above is Yikonglu's answer.

白Plain Chinese

颜渊、子路陪在孔子身边。孔子说：你们何不各自谈谈志向？子路说：我愿把车马、轻暖的皮袍拿出来和朋友一起用，用坏了也不心疼。颜渊说：我愿不夸耀自己的长处，不张扬自己的功劳。子路说：想听听老师的心愿。孔子说：让老人得到安顿，让朋友能够信赖，让年少的得到关怀。

A plain modern-Chinese rendering following Yikonglu's reading.

常The common reading

Why is Confucius' wish greater than those of Zilu and Yan Yuan?

注Key words in the commentary

“盍” — why not; “衣” is read in the departing tone, to wear; “敝” — worn out; “伐” — to boast; “施” — to make a show of. Zhu Xi gives two readings of “劳”: merit (as in “劳而不伐”) or toilsome tasks (not wishing to impose them on others), and says “亦通” — either works.

说What other commentators say

Zhu Xi: “老者养之以安，朋友与之以信，少者怀之以恩” — care for the old so they are at ease, deal with friends in good faith, cherish the young with kindness; another reading, “安之，安我也；信之，信我也；怀之，怀我也。亦通” — the old are at ease with me, friends trust me, the young turn to me, also works. He cites Master Cheng: “夫子安仁，颜渊不违仁，子路求仁” — the Master rests in ren, Yan Yuan does not depart from ren, Zilu seeks ren; “子路、颜渊、孔子之志，皆与物共者也，但有小大之差尔” — all three wishes are about sharing with others, differing only in scale; and “羁靮以御马而不以制牛……须要识得圣贤气象” — bridles are for horses, not oxen... one must learn to recognise the bearing of sages and worthies.
