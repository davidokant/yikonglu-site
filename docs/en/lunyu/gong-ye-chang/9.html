公冶长第五 · chapter 9Zai Yu slept in the daytime

What this chapter says

Yikonglu's reading: The point is not the nap but a slackened will and words that deeds do not match. “Not worth rebuking” is the heaviest rebuke. Confucius used the occasion to set a rule: hear the words, and also watch the conduct. Today: three gym memberships in a year, and not one visit. The common view is “Read as Confucius scolding a student far too harshly over a nap”; Yikonglu's reading puts the weight elsewhere.

白Plain Chinese

宰予大白天睡觉。孔子说：烂了的木头没法雕刻，粪土垒的墙没法抹平；对宰予，我还责备他什么呢？孔子又说：从前我看人，听了他的话就信他会照着做；如今我看人，听了他的话还要看他做没做。是宰予让我改了这个做法。

常The common reading

Read as Confucius scolding a student far too harshly over a nap.

注Key words in the commentary

“昼寝，谓当昼而寐” — daytime sleeping means sleeping in broad day; “朽，腐也。雕，刻画也。杇，镘也” — xiu is rotten, diao is to carve, wu (杇, read wū) is to plaster; “言其志气昏惰，教无所施也” — his will and spirit were dull and idle, so teaching had nothing to work on; “与，语辞。诛，责也。言不足责，乃所以深责之” — yu is a particle; zhu means to rebuke; saying he is not worth rebuking is the deepest rebuke; xing (行) in the falling tone; “宰予能言而行不逮，故孔子自言于予之事而改此失，亦以重警之也” — Zai Yu spoke well but his conduct fell short, so Confucius said that through Yu he had corrected this fault, also to warn him severely.

说What other commentators say

Hu Yin: “子曰疑衍文，不然，则非一日之言也” — the second “the Master said” may be an interpolation; otherwise these were words of different days. Fan Zuyu: “君子之于学，惟日孜孜，毙而后已……宰予昼寝，自弃孰甚焉” — the gentleman in learning is diligent every day until death; Zai Yu sleeping in the day — what self-abandonment is greater? Hu Yin: “宰予不能以志帅气，居然而倦。是宴安之气胜，儆戒之志惰也” — Zai Yu could not lead his vital spirit with his will and grew weary; the spirit of ease won and the will to vigilance slackened; “特因此立教，以警群弟子，使谨于言而敏于行耳” — Confucius merely used this to set a teaching and warn all his disciples to be careful in word and quick in deed.
