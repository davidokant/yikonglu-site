公冶长第五 · chapter 7Meng Wubo asked whether Zilu was humane

What this chapter says

Yikonglu's reading: Say plainly what visible ability there is; humaneness comes and goes, and one does not vouch for it. Three students, three talents: each line says what they can do, none says they are humane. Today: in a background check, the referee says “I vouch for his skills; his character I can't speak to”. The common view is “Confucius refusing to rate his students read as protectiveness or modesty”; Yikonglu's reading puts the weight elsewhere.

白Plain Chinese

孟武伯问：子路算得上仁吗？孔子说：不知道。他又问。孔子说：仲由嘛，一个出得起千辆兵车的大国，可以让他掌管兵事；仁不仁，我不知道。又问：冉求怎样？孔子说：冉求嘛，千户的大邑、百辆兵车的卿大夫之家，可以让他做总管；仁不仁，我不知道。又问：公西赤怎样？孔子说：公西赤嘛，系上礼带站在朝堂上，可以让他应对宾客；仁不仁，我不知道。

常The common reading

Confucius refusing to rate his students read as protectiveness or modesty.

注Key words in the commentary

Sheng (乘) read shèng; “赋，兵也。古者以田赋出兵，故谓兵为赋” — fu means troops; in antiquity troops were levied by land tax, so troops were called fu; “宰，邑长家臣之通号” — zai is the general title of a town head or household steward; chao (朝) read cháo; “子路之于仁，盖日月至焉者，或在或亡，不能必其有无，故以不知告之” — Zilu reached humaneness for a day or a month, sometimes present, sometimes absent, and it could not be guaranteed, so Confucius answered “I do not know”; “言子路之才，可见者如此，仁则不能知也” — he spoke of what Zilu's ability visibly was; humaneness could not be known.
