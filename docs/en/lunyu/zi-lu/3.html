Zi Lu (Book 13) · chapter 3必也正名

What this chapter says

一孔之见：正名先是一件实事：卫国父子争位，名分乱了；由此推开，才是名和实要对得上。今例：名片印“副总”的没人给权，叫“总监”的管着副总的事，底下人不知听谁的。常有人问：卫国等着孔子治理，他为什么开口先说“正名”？上面是一孔录的回答。

白Plain Chinese

子路问，卫君等着您主持政事，您先做什么，孔子说，先端正名分吧；子路嫌这太迂远，孔子说，仲由真粗率，不知道的事应当暂留不说。名分不正，言语便不合道理，事情就办不成，礼乐不能兴，刑罚失当，百姓便不知如何举措，所以君子所定的名必须说得通，说出的话必须行得通，一言也不可苟且。

Connections

Other books
  • Read alongside (Yikonglu)Three Character Classic 22: The word “usurped” (篡) in “Wang Mang usurped” (王莽篡) is the label historians fixed on him. Once the name is settled, right and wrong are clear.
  • Read alongside (Yikonglu)Three Character Classic 23: Historians argued over which of the Three Kingdoms was legitimate, and what they were arguing about was names and roles.
  • Read alongside (Yikonglu)Three Character Classic 25: The text says “received the abdication of Zhou” (受周禅), while commentators write “usurped” (篡): the dispute is between name and reality.
  • Read alongside (Yikonglu)Daodejing 32: “Once regulation begins there are names; once there are names, one should know when to stop” (始制有名，名亦既有，夫亦将知止). Confucius wants names rectified, and Laozi says that once names are established, one must know when to stop.
  • Cited by commentatorsDaodejing 57: Su Zhe explains "The more laws and orders are displayed" (法令滋彰) by citing Confucius's passage on rectifying names: "the people will not know where to put hand or foot."
  • Cited by commentatorsDaodejing 74: Su Zhe explains "The people do not fear death" (民不畏死), again citing Confucius: "the people will not know where to put hand or foot."

常The common reading

卫国等着孔子治理，他为什么开口先说“正名”？

注Key words in the commentary

“中”读去声。“卫君，谓出公辄也”；“是时出公不父其父而祢其祖，名实紊矣，故孔子以正名为先”；“迂，谓远于事情，言非今日之急务也”；“野，谓鄙俗。责其不能阙疑，而率尔妄对也”。

说What other commentators say

谢氏“正名虽为卫君而言，然为政之道，皆当以此为先”；杨氏“名不当其实，则言不顺。言不顺，则无以考实而事不成”。
