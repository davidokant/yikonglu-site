The Analects · Zhu Xi's commentaryCommonly Misread Characters

Some characters in the Analects are read differently from their everyday modern pronunciation, and Zhu Xi's commentary (Lunyu jizhu) marks the sound on the page. This list has 77 of them in book order. Each entry gives an example sentence, Zhu Xi's gloss in his own words, and the scanned page, with the old fanqie and tone terms explained. Readings match Yikonglu's recordings.

One entry per character and reading; the example is the chapter where Zhu Xi glosses it, and the other chapters with the same reading follow. Every entry has been checked against the base text (Zhu Xi, Lunyu jizhu, Sibu beiyao edition); click the page number to see it.

Where our recordings differ from Zhu Xi

For these 3 characters our recordings do not follow Zhu Xi's gloss. Both readings are given; they are not part of the list above.

长chángZhu Xi zhàng

Example必有寝衣，长一身有半。（Analects 10.6）

Zhu Xi“长，去声。” p. 134

How to readZhu Xi: qusheng, i.e. zhàng; our recordings read cháng.

拖tuōZhu Xi tuǒ

Example疾，君视之，东首，加朝服，拖绅。（Analects 10.13）

Zhu Xi“拖，徒我反。” p. 138

How to readZhu Xi: fanqie 徒我反, i.e. tuǒ; our recordings read tuō, the modern reading.

沮jǔZhu Xi jū

Example长沮、桀溺耦而耕，孔子过之，使子路问津焉。（Analects 18.6）

Zhu Xi“沮，七余反。” p. 246

How to readZhu Xi: fanqie 七余反, i.e. jū; our recordings read jǔ, the modern reading.
