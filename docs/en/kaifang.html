What is open, and what is not

Everything Yikonglu writes is open with credit; videos, narration, original characters and illustrations are not.

Open (CC BY 4.0: credit is all we ask, commercial use allowed)

  • Original text and textual variants
  • Plain renderings
  • Our readings (“Yikonglu reads it”, the text on the chapter pages)
  • Citation tables (source and base-edition page of every quotation)
  • Chapter-by-chapter index of commentaries with page numbers
  • Corrections log
  • Table of people
  • Table of topics

Not open

  • Quotations from modern commentaries (Yang Bojun, Qian Mu and others)
  • Videos, narration, original characters and illustrations

Page images: the source books are public-domain old editions, so we add no licence of our own and anyone may use them; please keep the seal and the “Yikonglu yikonglu.com · volume/page” line in the corner so readers can come back and check.

Commercial use, including model training and redistribution, is allowed; just credit “Yikonglu yikonglu.com” with a link to the original. Questions:
