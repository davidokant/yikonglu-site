What is open, and what is not

The groundwork for checking us is open; Yikonglu's own readings and editorial work are not.

Open (CC BY-NC 4.0: attribution, non-commercial)

  • Original text and textual variants
  • Plain renderings
  • Pronunciation tables
  • Citation tables (source and base-edition page of every quotation)
  • Chapter-by-chapter index of commentaries with page numbers
  • Corrections log
  • Table of people
  • Table of topics

Not open

  • Full reading notes
  • Collected “Yikonglu reads it” notes
  • Annotated page images
  • Indexes arranged by life situation
  • Videos, narration, characters and illustrations
