Part 3 · Knowing numbers · lecture 10 · 2 groups · not yet coveredEight sounds, four tones

TrTranslation

匏土革，木石金，絲與竹，乃八音。
Gourd, clay and hide, wood, stone and metal, silk and bamboo: these are the eight sounds.

曰平上，曰去入，此四聲，宜調協。
The level and the rising, the departing and the entering: these four tones should be kept in harmony.

Draft translation; it will be revised when the lecture is filmed.

From the Father's Lecture Notes

Both groups are about sound: one the sound of instruments, one the sound of speech. The eight sounds are not eight pitches but eight materials; an instrument belongs to whatever it is made of. The four tones are the rise and fall of speech, which the child uses every day without being told it has a name.

Translated from Yikonglu's Father's Lecture Notes for the Three Character Classic, written for a parent teaching a child.

Compared with Wang Xiang's edition

Group 26　曰平上，曰去入，此四聲，宜調協。
Not in Wang Xiang's edition. The group on the four tones was added in later popular editions; the lecture notes follow those.
