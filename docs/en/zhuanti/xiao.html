1 books · 14 chapters孝

问孝的人很多，答法各异；《孝经》是整本讲它。

Each topic is divided by book and lists the chapters whose original text mentions it: chapters we have covered include our reading; others show the original only.

The Analects14 chapters

孔子按各人的毛病答，所以同一个问题有好几种答法。朱熹注“善事父母为孝”。

  1. 1.2Filial and fraternal duty as the root of ren

    有子曰：其为人也孝弟，而好犯上者，鲜矣；不好犯上，而好作乱者，未之有也。君子务本，本立而道生。孝弟也者，其为仁之本与！

    Filial and fraternal duty is where practising ren begins. Today: kind to strangers online, no patience for one's parents at home — that is starting from the far end.

  2. 1.6A young man at home is filial

    子曰：弟子入则孝，出则弟，谨而信，汎爱众，而亲仁。行有余力，则以学文。

    Filial and brotherly duty, care and good faith, love for all come first, study of letters after: this is about order, not choosing one over the other. Today: a very capable person who never reads may do nothing wrong, but the day he does, he has nothing to check himself against.

  3. 1.11While the father lives, observe the son's intent

    子曰：父在，观其志；父没，观其行；三年无改于父之道，可谓孝矣。

    Change what is wrong at once; let what can wait, wait. That is time given to what one cannot yet bear to let go. Today: a son inherits his father's small factory. The old rule that cheated customers he scraps the next day; another he never liked he leaves for three years, not because it is right, but because he still can't let it go.

  4. 2.5Meng Yizi asks about filial piety

    孟懿子问孝。子曰：无违。樊迟御，子告之曰：孟孙问孝于我，我对曰无违。樊迟曰：何谓也？子曰：生，事之以礼；死，葬之以礼，祭之以礼。

    Today: borrowing money to throw a lavish birthday banquet is “doing what one may not”.

  5. 2.6Meng Wubo asks about filial piety

    孟武伯问孝。子曰：父母唯其疾之忧。

    Parents love their children and fear above all that they fall ill, so children should take their parents' heart as their own and look after their health. Today: staying up all night, drinking, riding without a helmet: you think it's your own business, but on Zhu Xi's reading it is exactly what your parents fear most.

  6. 2.7Ziyou asks about filial piety

    子游问孝。子曰：今之孝者，是谓能养。至于犬马，皆能有养；不敬，何以别乎？

    Merely feeding one's parents is no different from keeping dogs and horses; the difference lies in one word, reverence. Today: sending your parents money on time every month, not a cent short, yet on the phone it's always “yes, yes, I know”: the feeding is done, the reverence hasn't kept up.

  7. 2.8Zixia asks about filial piety (the hard part is the face)

    子夏问孝。子曰：色难。有事，弟子服其劳；有酒食，先生馔，曾是以为孝乎？

    All the work was done; the face never arrived.

  8. 2.20Approach them with dignity

    季康子问：使民敬、忠以劝，如之何？子曰：临之以庄则敬，孝慈则忠，举善而教不能则劝。

    Ji Kangzi asks for a method; Confucius answers with duty. Treating duty as a method goes wrong again.

  9. 2.21Why do you not take part in government?

    或谓孔子曰：子奚不为政？子曰：书云：孝乎！惟孝友于兄弟，施于有政。是亦为政，奚其为为政？

    The words are a pretext, and the point is also true.

  10. 4.20For three years not altering his father's way

    子曰：三年无改于父之道，可谓孝矣。

    The half sentence must be read with the half before it; “do not alter for three years” cannot be taken alone as an iron rule. Today: when young we remember only half of what our parents said, and recall the other half only after running a household ourselves.

  11. 8.21禹吾无间然矣not yet covered

    子曰：禹，吾无间然矣。菲饮食，而致孝乎鬼神；恶衣服，而致美乎黻冕；卑宫室，而尽力乎沟洫。禹，吾无间然矣。

  12. 11.4孝哉闵子骞not yet covered

    子曰：孝哉闵子骞！人不间于其父母昆弟之言。

  13. 13.20何如斯可谓之士矣子曰行己有耻not yet covered

    子贡问曰：何如斯可谓之士矣？子曰：行己有耻，使于四方，不辱君命，可谓士矣。曰：敢问其次。曰：宗族称孝焉，乡党称弟焉。曰：敢问其次。曰：言必信，行必果，硁硁然小人哉！抑亦可以为次矣。曰：今之从政者何如？子曰：噫！斗筲之人，何足算也。

  14. 19.18吾闻诸夫子孟庄子之孝也not yet covered

    曾子曰：吾闻诸夫子：孟庄子之孝也，其他可能也；其不改父之臣，与父之政，是难能也。
