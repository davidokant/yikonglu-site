Level 3 · 友18 chapters: read one to light it up, read them all to clear it

交友是修身的一部分。

Each topic is divided by book and lists the chapters whose original text mentions it: chapters we have covered include our reading; others show the original only.

The Analects18 chapters

曾子每天问自己“与朋友交而不信乎”，孔子说“无友不如己者”。收“友”“朋”两个字。

  1. 1.1Learning and practising it in season

    子曰：学而时习之，不亦说乎？有朋自远方来，不亦乐乎？人不知而不愠，不亦君子乎？

    What you learned, keep taking out to practise and to do. Today: watching the tutorial ten times is watching another bird fly.

  2. 1.4Three things I examine myself on daily

    曾子曰：吾日三省吾身：为人谋而不忠乎？与朋友交而不信乎？传不习乎？

    When you act for someone, did you hold anything back? Today: a colleague asks you to look over a plan; you can't be bothered to point out the flaw and say “looks good”.

  3. 1.7Honouring the worthy over good looks

    子夏曰：贤贤易色，事父母能竭其力，事君能致其身，与朋友交言而有信。虽曰未学，吾必谓之学矣。

    Someone who never studied but does all this — Zixia says he has studied. Today: the elders who had only a few years of schooling.

  4. 1.8A gentleman without weight has no dignity

    子曰：君子不重则不威，学则不固。主忠信。无友不如己者。过则勿惮改。

    Zhu Xi takes the words as they stand and does not talk his way around them. Today: the one person willing to tell you to your face tells you twice; if you don't change, the third time he says nothing.

  5. 2.21Why do you not take part in government?

    或谓孔子曰：子奚不为政？子曰：书云：孝乎！惟孝友于兄弟，施于有政。是亦为政，奚其为为政？

    The words are a pretext, and the point is also true.

  6. 4.26Importune a ruler and you meet disgrace; importune a friend and you grow distant

    子游曰：事君数，斯辱矣；朋友数，斯疏矣。

    Persuasion has limits. When it does not get through, stop (with a ruler, leave); urging too often only pushes people away.

  7. 5.24Why did Confucius find excessive politeness shameful?

    子曰：巧言、令色、足恭，左丘明耻之，丘亦耻之。匿怨而友其人，左丘明耻之，丘亦耻之。

    Both shames are a gap between inside and outside: sweet words, smiles and excessive courtesy to curry favour; befriending someone while nursing a grudge is pretence. Xie says it is worse than theft, because the heart is not upright. Today: holding a grudge, yet being the first at the table to raise a glass to him.

  8. 5.25Why was Confucius' wish greater than Zilu's and Yan Yuan's?

    颜渊、季路侍。子曰：盍各言尔志？子路曰：愿车马、衣轻裘，与朋友共。敝之而无憾。颜渊曰：愿无伐善，无施劳。子路曰：愿闻子之志。子曰：老者安之，朋友信之，少者怀之。

    All three share with others, differing in scale: Zilu shares carriage and horses with friends, Yan Yuan does not boast or show off, Confucius lets each find their place, like heaven and earth nurturing without a trace. Today: the auntie in the housing block whom the elderly trust with their keys and to whose door the children run first after school.

  9. 8.5Able, yet asking those less ablenot yet covered

    曾子曰：以能问于不能，以多问于寡；有若无，实若虚；犯而不校。昔者吾友尝从事于斯矣。

  10. 9.24Hold loyalty and good faith as primarynot yet covered

    子曰：主忠信，毋友不如己者，过则勿惮改。

  11. 10.14When a friend died with no one to take charge, he said: Let the…not yet covered

    朋友死，无所归。曰：于我殡。朋友之馈，虽车马，非祭肉，不拜。

  12. 12.23Zigong asked about friendshipnot yet covered

    子贡问友。子曰：忠告而善道之，不可则止，毋自辱焉。

  13. 12.24The gentleman gathers friends through culture, and through…not yet covered

    曾子曰：君子以文会友，以友辅仁。

  14. 13.28What must one be like to be called a scholar?not yet covered

    子路问曰：何如斯可谓之士矣？子曰：切切、偲偲、怡怡如也，可谓士矣。朋友切切、偲偲，兄弟怡怡。

  15. 15.9Zigong asked about practicing humanenessnot yet covered

    子贡问为仁。子曰：工欲善其事，必先利其器。居是邦也，事其大夫之贤者，友其士之仁者。

  16. 16.4There are three kinds of friends who help and three who harmnot yet covered

    孔子曰：益者三友，损者三友。友直，友谅，友多闻，益矣。友便辟，友善柔，友便佞，损矣。

  17. 16.5There are three kinds of enjoyment that help and three that harmnot yet covered

    孔子曰：益者三乐，损者三乐。乐节礼乐，乐道人之善，乐多贤友，益矣。乐骄乐，乐佚游，乐宴乐，损矣。

  18. 19.15My friend Zhang does what is hard to donot yet covered

    子游曰：吾友张也，为难能也。然而未仁。
