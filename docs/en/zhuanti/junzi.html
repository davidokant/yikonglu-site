“君子”本指有位的人，孔子多用来说有德的人。

Each topic is divided by book and lists the chapters whose original text mentions it: chapters we have covered include our reading; others show the original only.

常和“小人”对着讲。朱熹注“君子，成德之名”。

  1. 1.1Learning and practising it in season

    子曰：學而時習之，不亦說乎？有朋自遠方來，不亦樂乎？人不知而不慍，不亦君子乎？

    What you learned, keep taking out to practise and to do. Today: watching the tutorial ten times is watching another bird fly.

  2. 1.2Filial and fraternal duty as the root of ren

    有子曰：其為人也孝弟，而好犯上者，鮮矣；不好犯上，而好作亂者，未之有也。君子務本，本立而道生。孝弟也者，其為仁之本與！

    Filial and fraternal duty is where practising ren begins. Today: kind to strangers online, no patience for one's parents at home — that is starting from the far end.

  3. 1.8A gentleman without weight has no dignity

    子曰：君子不重則不威，學則不固。主忠信。無友不如己者。過則勿憚改。

    Zhu Xi takes the words as they stand and does not talk his way around them. Today: the one person willing to tell you to your face tells you twice; if you don't change, the third time he says nothing.

  4. 1.14A gentleman does not seek to eat his fill

    子曰：君子食無求飽，居無求安，敏於事而慎於言，就有道而正焉，可謂好學也已。

    Not going hungry on purpose; simply too occupied to mind.

  5. 2.12The gentleman is not a vessel

    子曰：君子不器。

    Virtue made whole first, then it can be used anywhere, not learning a hundred trades. Today: an accountant who keeps excellent books has her “vessel”; but when colleagues quarrel or a newcomer slips up, people come to her for advice, and that is more than a vessel.

  6. 2.13Act first, then speak

    子貢問君子。子曰：先行其言，而後從之。

    You still speak — only afterwards.

  7. 2.14Inclusive, not partisan

    子曰：君子周而不比，小人比而不周。

    Same dishes on the table, different reasons for the dinner.

  8. 3.7The gentleman does not compete

    子曰：君子無所爭，必也射乎！揖讓而升，下而飲。其爭也君子。

    The target does not lie; what is contested is whether your own hand is steady.

  9. 3.24The border warden of Yi asks to see him

    儀封人請見，曰：君子之至於斯也，吾未嘗不得見也。從者見之。出曰：二三子何患於喪乎？天下之無道也久矣，天將以夫子為木鐸。

    A minor official saw Confucius's weight at first sight. Today: an old teacher moved off the podium runs free classes in the community, and more people come than were ever in his class.

  10. 4.5Wealth and rank are what people want

    子曰：富與貴，是人之所欲也；不以其道得之，不處也。貧與賤，是人之所惡也；不以其道得之，不去也。君子去仁，惡乎成名？君子無終食之間違仁，造次必於是，顛沛必於是。

    Do not take wealth that should not come, and do not dodge an undeserved poverty by crooked means; the big choices and the small daily work make each other. Today: the bus brakes hard, and the young man's first grab is to steady the old woman beside him.

  11. 4.10No fixed yes, no fixed no; side with what is right

    子曰：君子之於天下也，無適也，無莫也，義之與比。

    Do not decide in advance and do not refuse in advance; look only at whether it accords with what is right. “Nothing allowed, nothing forbidden” without rightness in charge is mere recklessness. Today: a line judge does not decide beforehand who should win, but bends down to see whether this one ball touched the line.

  12. 4.11The gentleman cherishes virtue, the petty man his comfort

    子曰：君子懷德，小人懷土；君子懷刑，小人懷惠。

    “Cherishing one's ground” is not homesickness; it is a heart that guards only its own little comfort. The whole difference between gentleman and petty man lies between public and private. Today: two people receive the same job offer in another city; one asks first what he can learn and whom he can help, the other only which place is more comfortable to stay in.

  13. 4.16The gentleman understands rightness; the small man understands profit

    子曰：君子喻於義，小人喻於利。

    Both sides use the same kind of “understanding”; the difference is which thing they have understood through and through. Today: someone selling a second-hand item finds a scratch not in the listing, and changes “90% new” to “70% new” on their own.

  14. 4.24The gentleman wishes to be slow in speech and quick in action

    子曰：君子欲訥於言而敏於行。

    Of the easy end and the hard end, rein in the easy one (speaking) and spur on the hard one (doing). It is not refusing to speak, but not talking loosely.

  15. 5.2Of Zijian the Master said: A gentleman indeed is this man

    子謂子賤：君子哉若人！魯無君子者，斯焉取斯？

    A person's virtue is taken from the worthies around him, so praising him means also praising those who made him. Today: a newcomer wins an award and the first thing said on stage is to point to a teacher in the audience.

  16. 5.15Zichan had four ways of the gentleman

    子謂子產：有君子之道四焉：其行己也恭，其事上也敬，其養民也惠，其使民也義。

    Praising point by point actually shows there is still somewhere he has not reached. Respectful to himself, reverent to superiors, kind to the people, just in employing them: each of the four has its own object. Judging a person by one phrase or one event is wrong. Today: don't judge a small shop open for thirty years by one bad review.

  17. 6.3Why did Confucius give one man little grain and another a lot?

    子華使於齊，冉子為其母請粟。子曰：與之釜。請益。曰：與之庾。冉子與之粟五秉。子曰：赤之適齊也，乘肥馬，衣輕裘。吾聞之也，君子周急不繼富。原思為之宰，與之粟九百，辭。子曰：毋！以與爾鄰里鄉黨乎！

    How much to give depends not on favour but on what is right. Zihua's family rode fat horses and wore light furs, not in need, so he gave only a little, refusing to add to the rich; Yuan Si's salary was owed by his post, and refusing it would be wrong, so any excess should go to his neighbours. One case is “help the needy, not the rich”; the other is “a regular salary should not be declined”.

  18. 6.11Both are scholars: where is the difference?

    子謂子夏曰：女為君子儒，無為小人儒。

    Studying the same books, one who learns to make something of himself is a gentleman scholar; one who learns to be seen and to gain advantage is a petty scholar. Profit is not only money; reputation counts too.

  19. 6.16Too rough or too polished: which would Confucius prefer?

    子曰：質勝文則野，文勝質則史。文質彬彬，然後君子。

    Too much plainness is crude, too much ornament is hollow; only the two in balance make a gentleman. If one must lean, better lean plain: with the base intact, refinement can be added; with the base gone, refinement has nowhere to go.

  20. 6.24井有仁焉

    宰我問曰：仁者，雖告之曰：井有仁焉，其從之也？子曰：何為其然也？君子可逝也，不可陷也；可欺也，不可罔也。

    仁者急着救人，但站在井上才救得了人；合情理的话能骗他去井边，不合理的事蒙不住他。今例：电话里说家人出事要马上转账，她挂了电话先打给家里人。

  21. 6.25博学于文，约之以礼

    子曰：君子博學於文，約之以禮，亦可以弗畔矣夫！

    学要广，守要简；只博不约就漫成一片没边际。今例：收藏夹里存了几百节课，哪一节都没看完。

    佛肸召，子欲往。子路曰：昔者由也聞諸夫子曰：親於其身為不善者，君子不入也。佛肸以中牟畔，子之往也，如之何！子曰：然。有是言也。不曰堅乎，磨而不磷；不曰白乎，涅而不緇。吾豈匏瓜也哉？焉能繫而不食？

  71. 17.21Three years of mourning — a year is already long enoughnot yet covered

    宰我問：三年之喪，期已久矣。君子三年不為禮，禮必壞；三年不為樂，樂必崩。舊穀既沒，新穀既升，鑽燧改火，期可已矣。子曰：食夫稻，衣夫錦，於女安乎？曰：安。女安則為之！夫君子之居喪，食旨不甘，聞樂不樂，居處不安，故不為也。今女安，則為之！宰我出。子曰：予之不仁也！子生三年，然後免於父母之懷。夫三年之喪，天下之通喪也。予也有三年之愛於其父母乎？

  72. 17.23Does the gentleman esteem courage?not yet covered

    子路曰：君子尚勇乎？子曰：君子義以為上。君子有勇而無義為亂，小人有勇而無義為盜。

  73. 17.24Does the gentleman also have things he hates?not yet covered

    子貢曰：君子亦有惡乎？子曰：有惡：惡稱人之惡者，惡居下流而訕上者，惡勇而無禮者，惡果敢而窒者。曰：賜也亦有惡乎？惡徼以為知者，惡不孫以為勇者，惡訐以為直者。

  74. 18.7Zilu, following the Master, fell behind and met an old man…not yet covered

    子路從而後，遇丈人，以杖荷蓧。子路問曰：子見夫子乎？丈人曰：四體不勤，五穀不分。孰為夫子？植其杖而芸。子路拱而立。止子路宿，殺雞為黍而食之，見其二子焉。明日，子路行以告。子曰：隱者也。使子路反見之。至則行矣。子路曰：不仕無義。長幼之節，不可廢也；君臣之義，如之何其廢之？欲潔其身，而亂大倫。君子之仕也，行其義也。道之不行，已知之矣。

  75. 18.10The Duke of Zhou said to the Duke of Lu: The gentleman does not…not yet covered

    周公謂魯公曰：君子不施其親。不使大臣怨乎不以。故舊無大故，則不棄也。無求備於一人。

  76. 19.3Zixia's disciples asked Zizhang about making friendsnot yet covered

    子夏之門人問交於子張。子張曰：子夏云何？對曰：子夏曰：可者與之，其不可者拒之。子張曰：異乎吾所聞：君子尊賢而容眾，嘉善而矜不能。我之大賢與，於人何所不容？我之不賢與，人將拒我，如之何其拒人也？

  77. 19.4Even minor arts surely have something worth seeingnot yet covered

    子夏曰：雖小道，必有可觀者焉；致遠恐泥，是以君子不為也。

  78. 19.7The hundred artisans stay in their workshops to complete their…not yet covered

    子夏曰：百工居肆以成其事，君子學以致其道。

  79. 19.9The gentleman has three aspectsnot yet covered

    子夏曰：君子有三變：望之儼然，即之也溫，聽其言也厲。

  80. 19.10The gentleman wins the trust of his people before he works themnot yet covered

    子夏曰：君子信而後勞其民，未信則以為厲己也；信而後諫，未信則以為謗己也。

  81. 19.12Zixia's disciples and young followers are fine when it comes to…not yet covered

    子游曰：子夏之門人小子，當灑掃、應對、進退，則可矣。抑末也，本之則無。如之何？子夏聞之曰：噫！言遊過矣！君子之道，孰先傳焉？孰後倦焉？譬諸草木，區以別矣。君子之道，焉可誣也？有始有卒者，其惟聖人乎！

  82. 19.20Zhou's wickedness was not as extreme as they saynot yet covered

    子貢曰：紂之不善，不如是之甚也。是以君子惡居下流，天下之惡皆歸焉。

  83. 19.21The faults of the gentleman are like eclipses of the sun and…not yet covered

    子貢曰：君子之過也，如日月之食焉：過也，人皆見之；更也，人皆仰之。

  84. 19.25You are just being modestnot yet covered

    陳子禽謂子貢曰：子為恭也，仲尼豈賢於子乎？子貢曰：君子一言以為知，一言以為不知，言不可不慎也。夫子之不可及也，猶天之不可階而升也。夫子之得邦家者，所謂立之斯立，道之斯行，綏之斯來，動之斯和。其生也榮，其死也哀，如之何其可及也。

  85. 20.2Zizhang asked Confucius: What must one do to take part in…not yet covered

    子張問於孔子曰：何如斯可以從政矣？子曰：尊五美，屏四惡，斯可以從政矣。子張曰：何謂五美？子曰：君子惠而不費，勞而不怨，欲而不貪，泰而不驕，威而不猛。子張曰：何謂惠而不費？子曰：因民之所利而利之，斯不亦惠而不費乎？擇可勞而勞之，又誰怨？欲仁而得仁，又焉貪？君子無眾寡，無小大，無敢慢，斯不亦泰而不驕乎？君子正其衣冠，尊其瞻視，儼然人望而畏之，斯不亦威而不猛乎？子張曰：何謂四惡？子曰：不教而殺，謂之虐。不戒視成，謂之暴。慢令致期，謂之賊。猶之與人也，出納之吝；謂之有司。

  86. 20.3Without knowing fate, one cannot be a gentlemannot yet covered

    子曰：不知命，無以為君子也。不知禮，無以立也。不知言，無以知人也。
