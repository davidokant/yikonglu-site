Level 13 · 道59 chapters: read one to light it up, read them all to clear it

道家的道与儒家的道，字同义不同，这里并列着看。

Each topic is divided by book and lists the chapters whose original text mentions it: chapters we have covered include our reading; others show the original only.

The Analects59 chapters

“朝闻道，夕死可矣”“志于道”。“道”作“说”“引导”讲的章已剔。

  1. 1.2Filial and fraternal duty as the root of ren

    有子曰：其為人也孝弟，而好犯上者，鮮矣；不好犯上，而好作亂者，未之有也。君子務本，本立而道生。孝弟也者，其為仁之本與！

    Filial and fraternal duty is where practising ren begins. Today: kind to strangers online, no patience for one's parents at home — that is starting from the far end.

  2. 1.5Governing a state of a thousand chariots

    子曰：道千乘之國：敬事而信，節用而愛人，使民以時。

    Cheng Yi said these five things sound plain, but pushed to the limit, even Yao and Shun governed no differently. Today: a shop owner keeps every promise made to the staff, doesn't spend carelessly at month's end, and doesn't hand out private errands when people are busiest.

  3. 1.11While the father lives, observe the son's intent

    子曰：父在，觀其志；父沒，觀其行；三年無改於父之道，可謂孝矣。

    Change what is wrong at once; let what can wait, wait. That is time given to what one cannot yet bear to let go. Today: a son inherits his father's small factory. The old rule that cheated customers he scraps the next day; another he never liked he leaves for three years, not because it is right, but because he still can't let it go.

  4. 1.12In the use of ritual, harmony is prized

    有子曰：禮之用，和為貴。先王之道，斯為美，小大由之。有所不行，知和而和，不以禮節之，亦不可行也。

    Harmony alone, not regulated by ritual, does not work either. Today: in a team where no one wants to offend anyone, every meeting is all “looks good” and “fine by me”; the meetings are pleasant, and the work drifts further into a mess.

  5. 1.14A gentleman does not seek to eat his fill

    子曰：君子食無求飽，居無求安，敏於事而慎於言，就有道而正焉，可謂好學也已。

    Not going hungry on purpose; simply too occupied to mind.

  6. 2.3Guiding them by decrees

    子曰：道之以政，齊之以刑，民免而無恥；道之以德，齊之以禮，有恥且格。

    Punishments and decrees keep people from crime; virtue and ritual move them toward good. Today: the estate posts a notice, a 200-yuan fine for dumping rubbish, and the dumping stops; the day the camera breaks, the rubbish comes back.

  7. 3.16In archery, piercing the hide is not the point

    子曰：射不主皮，為力不同科，古之道也。

    The contest is in what can be trained, not in what you were born with.

  8. 3.24The border warden of Yi asks to see him

    儀封人請見，曰：君子之至於斯也，吾未嘗不得見也。從者見之。出曰：二三子何患於喪乎？天下之無道也久矣，天將以夫子為木鐸。

    A minor official saw Confucius's weight at first sight. Today: an old teacher moved off the podium runs free classes in the community, and more people come than were ever in his class.

  9. 4.5Wealth and rank are what people want

    子曰：富與貴，是人之所欲也；不以其道得之，不處也。貧與賤，是人之所惡也；不以其道得之，不去也。君子去仁，惡乎成名？君子無終食之間違仁，造次必於是，顛沛必於是。

    Do not take wealth that should not come, and do not dodge an undeserved poverty by crooked means; the big choices and the small daily work make each other. Today: the bus brakes hard, and the young man's first grab is to steady the old woman beside him.

  10. 4.8Hear the Way in the morning, die content at dusk

    子曰：朝聞道，夕死可矣。

    The hard part is not hearing it but truly believing it once heard; only one who has really gained something can say “I could die that evening content.” Today: an old man in a hospital bed finally understands what he never could all his life, holds his daughter's hand and says “now I'm at peace.”

  11. 4.9A scholar set on the Way who is ashamed of poor clothes

    子曰：士志於道，而恥惡衣惡食者，未足與議也。

    The mouth says the heart is set on the Way, but the heart is ordered about by things outside. The problem is not dressing poorly; it is treating poor dress as a shame. Today: someone who says he will devote himself to studying for the exam first spends three days picking the most expensive stationery.

  12. 4.15My Way is threaded through by one

    子曰：參乎！吾道一以貫之。曾子曰：唯。子出，門人問曰：何謂也？曾子曰：夫子之道，忠恕而已矣。

    “Loyalty and reciprocity” do not say the Way in full; they hand the learner something to hold. “Threaded through by one” is said only to the one who is ready to understand it. Today: an old craftsman tells the apprentice just one thing: do your own step through to the end, then think of the person who takes over next.

  13. 4.20For three years not altering his father's way

    子曰：三年無改於父之道，可謂孝矣。

    The half sentence must be read with the half before it; “do not alter for three years” cannot be taken alone as an iron rule. Today: when young we remember only half of what our parents said, and recall the other half only after running a household ourselves.

  14. 5.1Gongye Chang is fit to be given a wife

    子謂公冶長：可妻也。雖在縲紲之中，非其罪也。以其子妻之。子謂南容：邦有道不廢，邦無道免於刑戮。以其兄之子妻之。

    What matters is that the crime was not his; a prison imposed from outside is no measure of his honour. Today: someone was wrongly disciplined and later cleared; some people avoid him, and some still hand him the important jobs.

  15. 5.6If the Way does not prevail, I will take a raft out to sea

    子曰：道不行，乘桴浮於海。從我者，其由與？子路聞之喜。子曰：由也好勇過我，無所取材。

    Going out to sea is a sigh, a figure of speech; Zilu took it literally, with courage to spare but no sense of measuring what is fitting. Today: someone in the group chat says offhand they'll quit and travel the world, and someone else packs a bag that very night.

  16. 5.12The Master's outward cultivation can be heard

    子貢曰：夫子之文章，可得而聞也；夫子之言性與天道，不可得而聞也。

    “Wenzhang” is virtue showing in words and conduct; Confucius seldom spoke of nature and the Way of Heaven, and Zigong hearing it for the first time is admiration, not complaint. Today: after an apprentice has chopped vegetables in the kitchen for three years, the old chef finally talks to her about heat.

  17. 5.15Zichan had four ways of the gentleman

    子謂子產：有君子之道四焉：其行己也恭，其事上也敬，其養民也惠，其使民也義。

    Praising point by point actually shows there is still somewhere he has not reached. Respectful to himself, reverent to superiors, kind to the people, just in employing them: each of the four has its own object. Judging a person by one phrase or one event is wrong. Today: don't judge a small shop open for thirty years by one bad review.

  18. 5.20Why is this “foolishness” harder to learn than cleverness?

    子曰：寗武子邦有道則知，邦無道則愚。其知可及也，其愚不可及也。

    The “foolishness” is taking on what every clever person avoids, while still keeping himself safe and saving his lord; Master Cheng's mention of Bi Gan shows it is not simply feigning stupidity. Today: the year the company hit trouble everyone left, and she stayed to sort out the bad books.

  19. 6.10He said he lacked the strength: why didn't Confucius believe him?

    冉求曰：非不說子之道，力不足也。子曰：力不足者，中道而廢。今女畫。

    Someone truly lacking strength collapses halfway along the road; Ran Qiu drew a line on the ground before setting out. It is not that he cannot, but that he will not.

  20. 6.15Everyone goes out by the door: why not by the right way?

    子曰：誰能出不由戶？何莫由斯道也？

    Everyone knows to leave by the door rather than climb the wall; the right road in life lies right in front of us, yet people will not take it. It is not that the Way is far from them; they have wandered away themselves.

  21. 6.22齐一变至于鲁

    子曰：齊一變，至於魯；魯一變，至於道。

    强弱看的是眼前，离道远近看的是风俗底子；鲁只要把荒废的修起来，齐得先改掉图功利的习气。今例：门口喇叭喊打折排长队，对面老店不吆喝、称秤一两不少。

  22. 7.6志于道，据于德，依于仁，游于艺

    子曰：志於道，據於德，依於仁，遊於藝。

    四步有先后轻重：先立志，再守得住，再不离仁，余力在六艺里游，游也不是闲玩，是让心有处安放。今例：白天上班，晚上练一个钟头字，字不当饭吃，人不会散。

  23. 8.4Zengzi was ill and Meng Jingzi came to ask after himnot yet covered

    曾子有疾，孟敬子問之。曾子言曰：鳥之將死，其鳴也哀；人之將死，其言也善。君子所貴乎道者三：動容貌，斯遠暴慢矣；正顏色，斯近信矣；出辭氣，斯遠鄙倍矣。籩豆之事，則有司存。

  24. 8.7A scholar must be broad and resolute, for his burden is heavy…not yet covered

    曾子曰：士不可以不弘毅，任重而道遠。仁以為己任，不亦重乎？死而後已，不亦遠乎？

  25. 8.13Hold firmly to good faith and love learningnot yet covered

    子曰：篤信好學，守死善道。危邦不入，亂邦不居。天下有道則見，無道則隱。邦有道，貧且賤焉，恥也；邦無道，富且貴焉，恥也。

  26. 9.11The Master was gravely ill, and Zilu had disciples act as his…not yet covered

    子疾病，子路使門人為臣。病間，曰：久矣哉！由之行詐也，無臣而為有臣。吾誰欺？欺天乎？且予與其死於臣之手也，無寧死於二三子之手乎？且予縱不得大葬，予死於道路乎？

  27. 9.26Wearing a tattered padded robe and standing beside men in fox…not yet covered

    子曰：衣敝縕袍，與衣狐貉者立，而不恥者，其由也與？不忮不求，何用不臧？子路終身誦之。子曰：是道也，何足以臧？

  28. 9.29Some you can study with, but not go with toward the Waynot yet covered

    子曰：可與共學，未可與適道；可與適道，未可與立；可與立，未可與權。

  29. 11.19Zizhang asked about the way of the good mannot yet covered

    子張問善人之道。子曰：不踐跡，亦不入於室。

  30. 11.23Can Zhong You and Ran Qiu be called great ministers?not yet covered

    季子然問：仲由、冉求可謂大臣與？子曰：吾以子為異之問，曾由與求之問。所謂大臣者：以道事君，不可則止。今由與求也，可謂具臣矣。曰：然則從之者與？子曰：弒父與君，亦不從也。

  31. 12.19Ji Kangzi asked Confucius about government: What if I killed…not yet covered

    季康子問政於孔子曰：如殺無道，以就有道，何如？孔子對曰：子為政，焉用殺？子欲善，而民善矣。君子之德風，小人之德草。草上之風，必偃。

  32. 12.23Zigong asked about friendshipnot yet covered

    子貢問友。子曰：忠告而善道之，不可則止，毋自辱焉。

  33. 13.25The gentleman is easy to serve but hard to pleasenot yet covered

    子曰：君子易事而難說也：說之不以道，不說也；及其使人也，器之。小人難事而易說也：說之雖不以道，說也；及其使人也，求備焉。

  34. 14.1Xian asked about shame. The Master said: When the state has the…not yet covered

    憲問恥。子曰：邦有道，谷；邦無道，谷，恥也。

  35. 14.4When the state has the Way, be lofty in speech and lofty in…not yet covered

    子曰：邦有道，危言危行；邦無道，危行言孫。

  36. 14.20The Master spoke of Duke Ling of Wei's lack of the Waynot yet covered

    子言衛靈公之無道也，康子曰：夫如是，奚而不喪？孔子曰：仲叔圉治賓客，祝鮀治宗廟，王孫賈治軍旅。夫如是，奚其喪？

  37. 14.30The way of the gentleman has three parts, and I have achieved…not yet covered

    子曰：君子道者三，我無能焉：仁者不憂，知者不惑，勇者不懼。子貢曰：夫子自道也。

  38. 14.38Gongbo Liao slandered Zilu to Jisunnot yet covered

    公伯寮愬子路於季孫。子服景伯以告，曰：夫子固有惑志於公伯寮，吾力猶能肆諸市朝。子曰：道之將行也與？命也。道之將廢也與？命也。公伯寮其如命何！

  39. 15.6How upright Shi Yu is! When the state has the Way, he is like…not yet covered

    子曰：直哉史魚！邦有道，如矢；邦無道，如矢。君子哉蘧伯玉！邦有道，則仕；邦無道，則可卷而懷之。

  40. 15.24In my dealings with people, whom have I disparaged, whom have I…not yet covered

    子曰：吾之於人也，誰毀誰譽？如有所譽者，其有所試矣。斯民也，三代之所以直道而行也。

  41. 15.28It is people who can broaden the Way, not the Way that broadens…not yet covered

    子曰：人能弘道，非道弘人。

  42. 15.31The gentleman plans for the Way, not for foodnot yet covered

    子曰：君子謀道不謀食。耕也，餒在其中矣；學也，祿在其中矣。君子憂道不憂貧。

  43. 15.39Those whose ways differ do not make plans togethernot yet covered

    子曰：道不同，不相為謀。

  44. 15.41Music Master Mian came to see the Masternot yet covered

    師冕見，及階，子曰：階也。及席，子曰：席也。皆坐，子告之曰：某在斯，某在斯。師冕出。子張問曰：與師言之道與？子曰：然。固相師之道也。

  45. 16.2When the realm has the Way, ritual, music, and punitive…not yet covered

    孔子曰：天下有道，則禮樂征伐自天子出；天下無道，則禮樂征伐自諸侯出。自諸侯出，蓋十世希不失矣；自大夫出，五世希不失矣；陪臣執國命，三世希不失矣。天下有道，則政不在大夫。天下有道，則庶人不議。

  46. 16.5There are three kinds of enjoyment that help and three that harmnot yet covered

    孔子曰：益者三樂，損者三樂。樂節禮樂，樂道人之善，樂多賢友，益矣。樂驕樂，樂佚遊，樂宴樂，損矣。

  47. 16.11Seeing good, act as if you could not reach itnot yet covered

    孔子曰：見善如不及，見不善如探湯。吾見其人矣，吾聞其語矣。隱居以求其志，行義以達其道。吾聞其語矣，未見其人也。

  48. 17.4The Master went to Wucheng and heard the sound of strings and…not yet covered

    子之武城，聞絃歌之聲。夫子莞爾而笑，曰：割雞焉用牛刀？子游對曰：昔者偃也聞諸夫子曰：君子學道則愛人，小人學道則易使也。子曰：二三子！偃之言是也。前言戲之耳。

  49. 17.14To hear something on the road and repeat it along the way is to…not yet covered

    子曰：道聽而塗說，德之棄也。

  50. 18.2Liuxia Hui served as chief judge and was dismissed three timesnot yet covered

    柳下惠為士師，三黜。人曰：子未可以去乎？曰：直道而事人，焉往而不三黜？枉道而事人，何必去父母之邦？

  51. 18.6Changju and Jieni were ploughing side by sidenot yet covered

    長沮、桀溺耦而耕，孔子過之，使子路問津焉。長沮曰：夫執輿者為誰？子路曰：為孔丘。曰：是魯孔丘與？曰：是也。曰：是知津矣。問於桀溺，桀溺曰：子為誰？曰：為仲由。曰：是魯孔丘之徒與？對曰：然。曰：滔滔者天下皆是也，而誰以易之？且而與其從辟人之士也，豈若從辟世之士哉？耰而不輟。子路行以告。夫子憮然曰：鳥獸不可與同群，吾非斯人之徒與而誰與？天下有道，丘不與易也。

  52. 18.7Zilu, following the Master, fell behind and met an old man…not yet covered

    子路從而後，遇丈人，以杖荷蓧。子路問曰：子見夫子乎？丈人曰：四體不勤，五穀不分。孰為夫子？植其杖而芸。子路拱而立。止子路宿，殺雞為黍而食之，見其二子焉。明日，子路行以告。子曰：隱者也。使子路反見之。至則行矣。子路曰：不仕無義。長幼之節，不可廢也；君臣之義，如之何其廢之？欲潔其身，而亂大倫。君子之仕也，行其義也。道之不行，已知之矣。

  53. 19.2One who holds to virtue but not broadly, who believes in the…not yet covered

    子張曰：執德不弘，信道不篤，焉能為有？焉能為亡？

  54. 19.4Even minor arts surely have something worth seeingnot yet covered

    子夏曰：雖小道，必有可觀者焉；致遠恐泥，是以君子不為也。

  55. 19.7The hundred artisans stay in their workshops to complete their…not yet covered

    子夏曰：百工居肆以成其事，君子學以致其道。

  56. 19.12Zixia's disciples and young followers are fine when it comes to…not yet covered

    子游曰：子夏之門人小子，當灑掃、應對、進退，則可矣。抑末也，本之則無。如之何？子夏聞之曰：噫！言遊過矣！君子之道，孰先傳焉？孰後倦焉？譬諸草木，區以別矣。君子之道，焉可誣也？有始有卒者，其惟聖人乎！

  57. 19.19The Meng family made Yang Fu chief judge, and he asked Zengzi…not yet covered

    孟氏使陽膚為士師，問於曾子。曾子曰：上失其道，民散久矣。如得其情，則哀矜而勿喜。

  58. 19.22Gongsun Chao of Wei asked Zigong: From whom did Zhongni learn?not yet covered

    衛公孫朝問於子貢曰：仲尼焉學？子貢曰：文武之道，未墜於地，在人。賢者識其大者，不賢者識其小者，莫不有文武之道焉。夫子焉不學？而亦何常師之有？

  59. 19.25You are just being modestnot yet covered

    陳子禽謂子貢曰：子為恭也，仲尼豈賢於子乎？子貢曰：君子一言以為知，一言以為不知，言不可不慎也。夫子之不可及也，猶天之不可階而升也。夫子之得邦家者，所謂立之斯立，道之斯行，綏之斯來，動之斯和。其生也榮，其死也哀，如之何其可及也。
