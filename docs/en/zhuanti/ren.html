8 chapters仁

“仁”是《论语》里分量最重的一个字。孔子从不给它下一句定义，总是对不同的人、在不同的处境里说怎么做。朱熹说“仁者，爱之理，心之德也”。

Each topic lists the chapters whose original text mentions it: chapters we have covered include our reading; others show the original only. Uncovered chapters will be added automatically once the full text is online.

  1. 1.2Filial and fraternal duty as the root of ren

    有子曰：其为人也孝弟，而好犯上者，鲜矣；不好犯上，而好作乱者，未之有也。君子务本，本立而道生。孝弟也者，其为仁之本与！

    Filial and fraternal duty is where practising ren begins. Today: kind to strangers online, no patience for one's parents at home — that is starting from the far end.

  2. 1.3Clever words and a pleasing face

    子曰：巧言令色，鲜矣仁！

    Watch whether he does it to get the thing done, or only to be liked.

  3. 1.6A young man at home is filial

    子曰：弟子入则孝，出则弟，谨而信，汎爱众，而亲仁。行有余力，则以学文。

    This is about order, not about choosing one and dropping the other. Today: the capable person who never studied has nothing to check himself against when he is wrong.

  4. 3.3A person without humaneness — what of ritual?

    子曰：人而不仁，如礼何？人而不仁，如乐何？

    Fill the eight rows, sing the emperor's ode — if the person is wrong, ritual and music can do nothing with him.

  5. 4.1Living among the humane is beautiful

    子曰：里仁为美。择不处仁，焉得知？

    Choose the wrong place to live and what you lose is not the house but the heart that tells right from wrong. Today: when buying a flat people ask about schools, the metro and sunlight, and rarely about how the neighbours treat one another.

  6. 4.2The inhumane cannot long endure hardship

    子曰：不仁者不可以久处约，不可以长处乐。仁者安仁，知者利仁。

    Long hardship makes people overstep, long pleasure makes them indulge; whether outside things can take a person away depends on whether the original heart is there. Today: someone out of work for six months still gets up at seven, shaves, and goes out for a run.

  7. 4.3Only the humane can love and hate others

    子曰：唯仁者能好人，能恶人。

    Everyone can like and dislike; the “can” lies in doing it rightly, which requires a heart that is not tied to anything. Today: a competition judge who sees a former student take the stage stands up, leaves the panel and gives no score for that round.

  8. 4.4Set your heart on humaneness and there is no evil

    子曰：苟志于仁矣，无恶也。

    Resolve does not guarantee no mistakes; it guarantees no intention to do wrong. Today: a learner driver scrapes the car three times in a year but never once runs a red light.
